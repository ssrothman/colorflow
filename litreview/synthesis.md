# Synthesis: collective colour effects in high-energy QCD

_Status: after iteration 1 (15 papers read, 2026-10-30). This is an early, deliberately partial picture: it covers the founding theory of coherence and preconfinement, the classic e⁺e⁻ string effect, Tevatron initial–final coherence, LEP2 colour reconnection in WW, and colour flow/CR in tt̄ at the LHC. Not yet covered: modern CR models (QCD-based/beyond-leading-colour, rope/junction), small-system collectivity, rapidity gaps / colour-singlet exchange, beyond-leading-colour showers, the LEP-combined CR result, EIC prospects. Claims cite `summaries/<ID>.md`; numbers there are anchored to the papers, and all relevance-3 summaries have been independently verified._

## RQ1 — Physical origin of collective colour effects

Two distinct mechanisms keep appearing, and they should be kept apart:

1. **Perturbative soft-gluon coherence (interference).** Soft, long-wavelength gluons cannot resolve individual colour charges, so radiation from a system of partons is governed by its *colour topology*, not by independent emitters ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)). This has two faces:
   - *Intra-jet (RQ1a):* angular ordering of successive emissions, giving the hump-backed plateau in ln(1/x) and a reduced multiplicity growth ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)).
   - *Inter-jet (RQ1b, 1d, 1e):* radiation patterns are a sum of colour-connected "antennae"; regions between colour-*unconnected* partons are depleted. In qq̄g vs qq̄γ the soft density midway between q and q̄ is suppressed by 7/16 ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)); in hadron collisions the same decomposition links incoming and outgoing partons, so initial–final interference shapes where extra jets go ([Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md), [Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md)).
2. **Non-perturbative colour connection at hadronization.** Already at leading-log and large N_c the cascade is *planar*: final partons form adjacent colour-singlet clusters whose masses are power-damped to O(Q₀) independent of the hard scale (preconfinement) and that are local in space-time ([Amati_1979fg](summaries/Amati_1979fg.md), [Bassetto_1979vy](summaries/Bassetto_1979vy.md), [Marchesini_1980cr](summaries/Marchesini_1980cr.md)). Strings and clusters hadronize along these colour connections, so hadron flow inherits the colour topology. **Colour reconnection (CR)** is the question of whether this leading-N_c assignment is rearranged (e.g. between overlapping W decays, or between MPI systems) before hadronization ([L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md), [DELPHI_2006tie](summaries/DELPHI_2006tie.md), [Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)).

Both mechanisms rest on the **same leading-N_c colour-flow structure**, which is why the classic data cannot easily tell them apart (see RQ3). Local parton–hadron duality (LPHD) is the bridge that lets perturbative patterns be compared with hadron flows ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)).

## RQ2 — Theoretical results and models

- **Coherent parton showers.** Angular-ordered final-state showers with planar colour flow and cluster hadronization ([Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)); extension to incoming partons and to initial–final colour connections via emission cones bounded by colour-connected partners, with non-planar (1/N²) terms dropped and argued to be dynamically small, "few percent" ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md), from [Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md)).
- **Analytic coherence.** Antenna patterns, MLLA hump-backed plateau, LPHD ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)); an MLLA+LPHD calculation describes the W+jet soft-radiation pattern without any hadronization model ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)).
- **Colour reconnection models.** LEP2 analyses are expressed in Sjöstrand–Khoze SK-I (parameter κ / reconnection probability), SK-II, ARIADNE and HERWIG CR ([L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md), [DELPHI_2006tie](summaries/DELPHI_2006tie.md)). For tops, default Pythia 8 CR does not touch top-decay products; new "forced", "swap/move/flip" models give m_top shifts of up to −7 GeV at maximal strength and −0.6 to +0.36 GeV after tuning, and the authors argue ~500 MeV is a realistic CR uncertainty ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)).

## RQ3 — Experimental results and constraints

| Effect | Key result | Discriminating power |
|---|---|---|
| String/drag effect, e⁺e⁻ → 3 jets (RQ1b) | OPAL with lepton-tagged quark jets: q–g / q–q̄ particle-flow ratio R̃ = 1.62 ± 0.07 (8.9σ, stat. only) in geometrically equivalent regions; Jetset reproduces it, incoherent IF (Cojets) gives ≈1.02 ([OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md)) | Rules out a purely kinematic origin; **cannot** separate perturbative drag from Lund string |
| Initial–final coherence, p̄p → 3 jets (RQ1b,d) | CDF: 3rd-jet angular distributions favour HERWIG and initial–final-restricted PYTHIA, not ISAJET/standard PYTHIA 5.6 ([CDF_1994zkl](summaries/CDF_1994zkl.md)); D0: χ²/8 dof HERWIG 5.1/7.7, JETRAD O(αs³) 7.9/7.9, PYTHIA-AO 12.7/11.3, ISAJET 48/25, PYTHIA no-AO 77/63 ([D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md)) | Shape-only, detector-level; a fixed-order 2→3 calculation does nearly as well, so it mainly tests the wide-angle first emission |
| Jet vs boson (RQ1e) | D0 W+jet: soft activity around the jet exceeds that around the W, most in the event plane; R_sig ≈ 1.38 ± 0.10 (stat.), matched by PYTHIA-AO+string (≈1.30) and MLLA+LPHD (≈1.44), not by AO-off (≈1.11, ≈1.02) ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)) | 390 events: evidence, not precision |
| CR in e⁺e⁻ → WW (LEP2) | L3: R_N = 0.915 ± 0.023 ± 0.021, consistent with no CR, SK-I 100 % excluded at 4.9σ, P_reco < 64 % (95 % CL) ([L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md)); DELPHI: particle flow + m_W-estimator differences give κ = 2.2 (+2.5 −1.3), P_reco ≈ 52 % (31–68 %) ([DELPHI_2006tie](summaries/DELPHI_2006tie.md)) | Only SK-I-like models are constrained; SK-II, ARIADNE, HERWIG CR barely move R_N. Mild L3/DELPHI tension |
| Colour flow in tt̄ (LHC) | ATLAS unfolded jet pull: colour-octet W disfavoured (χ²/NDF ≈ 45/3); no generator fits all observables, Powheg+Pythia8 strongly excluded; CR tune variation 0.16–0.22 % per bin on the forward pull angle, comparable to other small systematics, with hadronisation (0.55 %) the largest ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)) | More sensitive to the shower/hadronization model than to CR strength as varied in the tune |

**Emerging pattern.** Coherence as a *perturbative* phenomenon is well established qualitatively across e⁺e⁻, p̄p and boson+jet final states, and coherence-free models fail. But the observables used were mostly shape-only, detector-level and statistics-limited, and they rarely isolate perturbative vs non-perturbative origin. CR is constrained only for specific models (SK-I) at LEP2, and its size in hadron collisions remains mainly a tuning/systematics question.

## RQ4 — Experimental tests proposed in the theory literature

- Soft-hadron azimuthal asymmetries in γ+jet, W+jet and gq→gq events, possibly allowing event-by-event subprocess tagging ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)); the W+jet variant was carried out by D0 ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)).
- Energy dependence of the hump-backed plateau peak in a restricted cone, the clean intra-jet coherence test (a cone cut alone produces a hump) ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)); peak scaling should show first for kaons and baryons ([Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)).
- Particle flow between jets in qg→qg vs W→qq̄ at hadron colliders as a colour-flow test and W-tagging discriminant ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md)).
- For CR in tt̄: charged multiplicity / ΣpT outside the b and W jets, ⟨pT⟩(n_ch), UE density from jet areas, cone multiplicity around the top direction; jet pull found *not* discriminating after tuning ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)).
- Many more proposals are queued (jet colour ring, dipolarity, H→bb̄ colour-flow radiation patterns, VBF Higgs, ZZ→bb̄cc̄ CR test, in-jet Λ_c/D⁰), to be read in later iterations.
