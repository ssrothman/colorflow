# Synthesis: collective colour effects in high-energy QCD

_Status: after iteration 3 (42 papers read, 2026-11-02). Covered so far: the origins of soft-gluon coherence and angular ordering, preconfinement, the Lund string picture of colour flow, the string/drag effect from PETRA to LEP-1 (incl. how the string and perturbative explanations relate), intra-jet coherence tests at LEP-1 (hump-backed plateau and its critique, two-particle angular correlations), Tevatron and LHC initial–final coherence, colour reconnection (CR) in WW from the first proposal (1988) through the SK/GH/ARIADNE models to the LEP-wide combination, CR models for hadron colliders (Pythia QCD-based, Herwig cluster CR, generalised area law), colour flow in tt̄ and jet-pull observables, and a first look at colour entanglement. Not yet covered: rope/junction models and small-system collectivity, rapidity gaps / colour-singlet exchange (beyond two model papers), beyond-leading-colour showers, EIC prospects, the LEP-1 string-effect and Z→3-jet CR papers, and Mueller 1981 / BCMM 1982. Claims cite `summaries/<ID>.md`; "our" marks the reviewers' assessment as opposed to the authors' claims._

## RQ1 — Physical origin of collective colour effects

Two mechanisms recur throughout. The literature itself stresses that at leading colour they make the same predictions, so most data cannot separate them.

### 1. Perturbative soft-gluon coherence (interference)

- **Origin.** Soft, wide-angle gluons cannot resolve the individual colour charges inside a jet; they are emitted coherently by the jet's total colour charge. Ermolaev–Fadin derived, in the double-log approximation (DLA), that the exclusive e⁺e⁻ → qq̄ + n g cross section factorises into a tree of eikonal emissions, each gluon emitting at successively smaller angles, with Casimir C_F or C_A per emitter. They stress that this holds only after *summing* diagrams ("a gauge in which only one diagram contributes does not exist"), so angular ordering is an interference result ([Ermolaev_1981cm](summaries/Ermolaev_1981cm.md)). Bassetto–Ciafaloni–Marchesini give the colour-operator form: the eikonal current J^μ = Σ T_i(…) acts in colour space, large-angle emission "measures the total colour charge" T_Jet, and crossed (interference) diagrams cancel the non-angular-ordered region order by order ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). Mueller 1981 is the parallel original derivation (queued, not read). The letter by Ermolaev–Fadin states its results without derivation, so the non-overlap and completeness of its regions are asserted, not shown ([Ermolaev_1981cm](summaries/Ermolaev_1981cm.md)).
- **Intra-jet consequences (RQ1a).** Angular ordering reduces the multiplicity anomalous dimension by 1/√2 relative to an incoherent ladder, giving n_jet ∝ (log Q²/Λ²)^{K_1} exp(K_0√log(Q²/Λ²)) with K_0 ≈ 1.7, K_1 ≈ 0.73, and a "hump-backed" Gaussian in ln(1/x) peaking at x ≈ (Q_0/Q)^{1/2}; a coherent MC shows the hump, a non-coherent one peaks at x ≈ Q_0/Q instead ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). The same picture underlies MLLA ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)) and the coherent showers ([Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)).
- **Inter-jet coherence (RQ1b, 1d, 1e) needs more than DLA.** DLA keeps only Casimir factors, so colour correlations between emitters, i.e. inter-jet interference, are absent at that accuracy ([Ermolaev_1981cm](summaries/Ermolaev_1981cm.md)); BCM find jets in different angular regions uncorrelated to double-log accuracy, with only single-log inter-jet correlations ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). The inter-jet pattern comes from the soft-gluon *antenna*: for qq̄g, W = N_c(H²₊₁ + H²₋₁) + (2C_F − N_c)H²₊₋, where the q–q̄ term carries a negative coefficient −1/N_c ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)). Midway between q and q̄ the qq̄g/qq̄γ density ratio is 7/16 ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)). BCM's closed-chain formula for multi-gluon emission from a colour singlet (nearest-neighbour 1/(ζ_{i1i2}ζ_{i2i3}⋯) products) is the conceptual root of dipole/antenna emission, though its colour exactness beyond two gluons is not demonstrated in the review ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). In hadron collisions the same decomposition links incoming and outgoing partons ([Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md), [Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md)). Two-gluon azimuthal correlations inside a jet (back-to-back emission suppressed by 7/16 at LO) have "the same dynamical origin as the string effect" according to OPAL ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)).

### 2. Non-perturbative colour connection at hadronization

- **Preconfinement.** In the angular-ordered, planar (large-N_c) cascade, colour-connected partons have Sudakov-damped mass spectra and form colour-singlet clusters ([Amati_1979fg](summaries/Amati_1979fg.md), [Bassetto_1979vy](summaries/Bassetto_1979vy.md), [Marchesini_1980cr](summaries/Marchesini_1980cr.md)). BCM's MC gives general clusters of a few Q_0, but the perturbative colourless qq̄ clusters are "rather large objects" (⟨M⟩ ≈ 10 GeV estimate), which they propose to cure by enhancing g → qq̄, anticipating forced splitting in cluster models ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)).
- **Lund string.** The colour field is stretched q → g → q̄; the gluon is a kink. The two string pieces fragment in their own rest frames and are boosted, so hadrons lie along two hyperbolae and the q–q̄ region is depleted: the **string effect** ([Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md), [Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)). The force on the kink is twice that on a quark, compared by the authors with the QCD ratio 2/(1−1/N²) = 9/4 (= C_A/C_F, our remark) ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)); our reading is that the string thus already encodes the colour-charge ratio driving the antenna pattern ([Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md)). An alternative topology (Montvay octet string + junction) interpolates between string and independent fragmentation; the Lund authors say data favour the kink or a large octet tension ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)).
- **Colour-topology ambiguity → colour reconnection.** Andersson et al. note already in 1983 that the field topology is not fixed by the charges (two ways in qq̄gg, six in gg → gg with octet remnants) and that separating amplitudes by colour configuration is unique only for N_c → ∞ ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)). **CR** asks whether the leading-colour assignment is rearranged before hadronization: between the two W's at LEP2 ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), [Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)), between MPI systems at hadron colliders, or with top-decay products ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md), [Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)).

### Key structural point: string and antenna coincide at leading colour

Dropping the O(1/N_c²) term, the qq̄g antenna becomes two incoherent qq̄-like dipoles (q–g, g–q̄), each boosted from its rest frame: "3 = 2 + 2 + Lorentz boosts", literally the Lund string mechanism. The authors conclude the two pictures "appear to be rather close" and that the string "catches quite well" the coherent perturbative physics ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)); Khoze et al. later call this "peaceful coexistence" ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). They differ only in (i) the energy and hadron-mass/p_out dependence (perturbative r tends to an energy-independent asymptote for all hadrons; the string boost gives a fixed-scale mass dependence) and (ii) the colour-suppressed negative q–q̄ term, present in pQCD but in neither the string nor leading-colour dipole showers ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md), [Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). These 1/N_c² effects were unobserved as of 1996 ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). The same logic applies to CR: in WW the perturbative reconnection is O(α_s²), colour-suppressed by 1/(N_c²−1) and cut off at ω ≲ Γ_W, and also contains negative-sign "dipoles" with no probabilistic counterpart ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). CR models are therefore attempts to model subleading-colour freedom non-perturbatively: by space-time string overlap ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)), by string-length (λ) minimisation ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md), [Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md), [Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)), by SU(3) algebra plus string-length minimisation ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)), or by cluster-mass reduction ([Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)).

### Colour entanglement

A third idea is soft colour exchange with spectators breaking TMD factorisation in hadron collisions (RQ1e). A PHENIX search found no clear signal; an apparent width decrease at 510 GeV was a selection bias, and properly binned widths follow standard (CSS) evolution ([Osborn_2018bwn](summaries/Osborn_2018bwn.md)).

## RQ2 — Theoretical results and models

### Coherence: analytic results and coherent showers
- **DLA/LLA foundations:** coherent branching with C_F/C_A per emitter and an angular-ordered Sudakov form factor ([Ermolaev_1981cm](summaries/Ermolaev_1981cm.md)); colour-operator factorisation, angular-ordered generating functional, multiplicity and hump-backed plateau, KNO dispersions (c_2 ≈ 1/3 gluon jet, ≈ 3/4 quark jet), and an O(√α_s) subleading shift of the peak toward small x that "may contribute … to make the experimental detection of the dip more difficult" ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)).
- **MLLA + LPHD:** antenna patterns and the hump-backed plateau ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)); MLLA predicts a ξ-peak slope b = 0.627 vs ln E_cm ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md)). LPHD is called the "Achilles heel": not derivable from QCD ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
- **Coherent showers:** angular-ordered final-state showers with planar colour flow and cluster hadronization ([Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)); extension to initial–final colour connections, dropping 1/N² terms ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md), [Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md)); colour-dipole cascade (ARIADNE), equivalent to a string with gluon kinks and leading-colour by construction ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md), [Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)). Azimov et al. argued in 1985 that the angular-ordered Marchesini–Webber MC "oversimplifies" inter-jet coherence ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)); JADE nevertheless found it reproduces the string effect (RQ3).

### The string effect: competing predictions
- **Lund (1980):** an event-level correlation of the gluon-jet multiplicity excess and the angular asymmetry, predicted "of the order of 16%"; at √s = 30 GeV (MC) Δn·Δθ > 0 in 60 % of target events (58 % with backgrounds) vs 50 % without the effect. This is a string-vs-independent-fragmentation test with no perturbative argument ([Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md)).
- **Perturbative antenna (1985):** for the JADE/TPC geometry, r = N₁₃/N₁₂ = 2.56 if jet 3 is always the gluon, 1.17 for an incoherent model that keeps the gluon's larger charge, 1.62 for the realistic gluon-assignment mixture; with the three-jet selection, r_PT = 1.50 (JADE) and 1.55 (TPC). Hence the effect comes from interference, not gluon dominance ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)).
- **Discriminating the two (1990):** ARIADNE + JETSET vs JETSET string only, symmetric 120° qq̄g. With the cascade r₁ falls with energy (2.74 at 30 GeV → 2.38 at 93 GeV → 2.13 at 750 GeV); string-only gives 2.69 → 2.99 → 2.89. The hadron-mass and p_out dependence of r is "drastically reduced already for LEP energies" with the cascade. The double-inclusive r₂ = ⟨N₂N₃⟩/⟨N₁N₃⟩ is below r₁ with the cascade (2.52 vs 2.74 at 30 GeV) but equal for string only ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)). Our assessment: these are MC-only statements, the two setups use different Lund parameters, and since ARIADNE drops the 1/N_c² term its r₂ < r₁ comes from perturbative spill-over, so the comparison is "string topology with vs without a cascade", not "string vs full pQCD" ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)).
- **Colour flow and "QCD measurements":** the colour-flow assumption changed CELLO's α_s from ≈ 0.17 (independent fragmentation) to ≈ 0.25 (Lund); the Lund authors concluded that separating perturbative QCD and fragmentation "is lost" at PETRA energies ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)).

### Colour-reconnection models (chronological)
- **Gustafson–Pettersson–Zerwas 1988 (origin).** W decay vertices ~0.1 fm apart ≪ 1 fm, so cross pairings qQ̄′, Qq̄′ can form singlets; naive colour counting gives 1/9 = 1/N_c². Type-I (bag-like) vacuum would enhance, type-II (thin vortex) suppress recoupling. As a 100 %-recoupled toy without gluon radiation before recoupling, multiplicity at α = 30° falls from 45.0 to 22.7 and the central region empties. The paper already warns that W-property measurements must consider this ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md)).
- **Sjöstrand–Khoze 1994 (SK-I/II/II′).** Perturbative CR is negligible: ΔN/N ≲ O(10⁻²), m_W effect ≲ 5 MeV. Non-perturbative CR via space-time string overlap: SK-I (Gaussian flux tubes, free k_I), SK-II (vortex lines, reconnect at first core crossing, no free strength), SK-II′ (only if string length decreases). With k_I = 0.6, "a realistic scenario with ~30 % reconnection" at 170 GeV; m_W shifts at 170 GeV of +14 to −10 MeV (I), −21 to −34 MeV (II), −17 to −31 MeV (II′); toy extremes up to +576 MeV. Best estimate ~40 MeV total CR uncertainty. Inclusive observables are nearly blind (⟨n_ch⟩ 36.63 → 36.31/36.45) ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)).
- **Gustafson–Häkkinen 1994.** Gluons with E ≳ Γ_W are radiated by each W before recoupling, so two parton chains (⟨n⟩ ≈ 4.3 gluons each) offer (n+1)(m+1) recoupling options; group theory only bounds the probability to 0.03 < P < 0.97, so 1/9 is "not a derived probability". Proposed dynamics: recouple to the configuration minimising string length λ, benchmark rate ~10 % (an input), ⟨Δλ⟩ ≈ 2 vs ≈ 0.7 for SK-I. They regard SK-I's strength as having "no dynamical motivation" ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)).
- **Lönnblad 1996 (ARIADNE CR).** Random colour index in [1,9] per dipole mimics 1/N_c²; equal-index dipoles swap if λ decreases, iterated after every emission (so partly perturbative). For WW, cross-talk only for E_g < Γ_W (the AR-2 prescription per DELPHI). m_W shift ≈ +32 MeV at 170 GeV (range +23 to +58 MeV, threshold-dependent), with MC-truth jet assignment. Intra-system CR at the Z is retunable but hardens p_⊥out. CR in the unordered dipole cascade gives far too few HERA rapidity-gap events and the wrong m_X shape ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)).
- **Later models.** Generalised area law, strength from HERA diffractive DIS, predicts up to 2× more Z gap events and Δm_W ≈ +65 MeV ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)). Pythia QCD-based CR fits LEP, LHC ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) and Λ/K⁰_S, predicts ~20× more charm/bottom baryons, fails identified-hadron p⊥ spectra and rapidity gaps ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)); CR is required for ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch), may mimic flow, and contributes up to ~0.5 GeV to m_top ([Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md)). Herwig++ cluster CR restores ATLAS ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) and dN/dη but fails diffraction-enhanced high-n_ch minimum bias ([Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)). Top-specific CR: tuned m_top shifts −0.6 to +0.36 GeV, ~500 MeV realistic uncertainty ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)); QCD-based CR calibrated on m_W gives smaller shifts ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)).
- **Our reading of the lineage.** Two families exist from 1994 on: space-time overlap (SK) and momentum-space λ-minimisation (GH → ARIADNE → GAL → Pythia QCD-based/gluon-move style) ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md), [Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)). λ-minimising models can only shorten strings and so only reduce multiplicity; SK-I allows ~20 % length-increasing crossings ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md), [Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)).

### Colour-flow observables
Jet pull (pull angle points to the colour partner; ~1.5× signal/background shape difference in simulated ZH→bb̄) ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md)); dipolarity for top tagging, generator-dependent gains ([Hook_2011cq](summaries/Hook_2011cq.md)).

**How the LEP2 outcome compares with the 1988–96 theory.**
- The qualitative SK prediction held: CR small in inclusive observables but a leading m_W(qqqq) systematic. SK's ~40 MeV estimate is close to the final 35 MeV, but our assessment is that the match is partly coincidental: LEP obtained 35 MeV only after soft-particle de-weighting, with k_I at its 68 % CL upper limit (2.10), and found shifts up to ~100 MeV with standard reconstruction ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)).
- LEP prefers roughly twice SK's reference strength (~51 % at 189 GeV vs ~30 % at 170 GeV), inside the factor-2 model uncertainty SK allowed; k_I values transfer only within the same PYTHIA implementation ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). Both are far above the 1/9 colour-counting baseline of [Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), consistent with GH's point that 1/9 does not fix the rate ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)).
- The striking event-by-event signals of the 1988 toy (multiplicity halved, empty centre) were revised away by the authors once hard-gluon emission before recoupling was included ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), [Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). The particle-flow ratio R_N that LEP did use descends from the inter-W depletion idea ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)); the m_W-estimator method is close to SK's "self-screening" suggestion ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)).
- ARIADNE's +32 MeV (MC-truth reconstruction) shrank to ΔM_W(std, cone) = 7.2 ± 4.1 MeV in DELPHI's realistic analysis, as Lönnblad expected ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)).

**Emerging pattern.**
1. Coherence-free models fail in every collider environment. In e⁺e⁻ the most convincing evidence comes from angular correlations ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md), [L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md)) and the string effect vs incoherent showers ([JADE_1985mmd](summaries/JADE_1985mmd.md)); the ξ spectrum, long cited as the flagship test, discriminates only weakly once string hadronization is retuned ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md), [Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
2. Whether inter-jet colour effects are "perturbative" or "string" remains experimentally open: the two coincide at leading colour, and the proposed discriminants (energy/mass dependence, r₂/r₁, 1/N_c² asymmetries) have not been seen to settle it ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md), [Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
3. At hadron colliders the best generators still miss precise unfolded data (CMS forward region), so "coherence is understood" is overstated.
4. CR is favoured at LEP2 (2.8σ, SK-I-specific) and needed to tune hadron-collider minimum bias, but no observable isolates it model-independently; its main practical impact is as a systematic on m_W and m_top.

## RQ4 — Experimental tests proposed in the theory literature

**Intra-jet coherence**
- Multiplicity growth, small-x depletion (look for depletion rather than the dip, which hadronization may fill), KNO dispersions, single/double-cone energy-flow form factors; also "higher energy p–p̄ collider jets" ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)).
- Energy dependence of the hump-backed peak in a restricted cone, kaons and baryons first ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)). Peak evolution done by OPAL + TASSO ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md)); the flavour dependence of identified-particle ξ spectra, absent in MLLA, is suggested as a further check by [Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md).
- EMMC (Dokshitzer et al.) and two-particle azimuthal correlations: done by OPAL ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)).

**Inter-jet coherence and the string effect**
- Lund gluon-tagging asymmetry Δn·Δθ ([Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md)); superseded in practice by the inter-jet population ratio ([JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md)).
- qq̄g vs qq̄γ (the negative "third antenna") and the string effect with heavy-quark-tagged quark jets ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)); done by DELPHI ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)) and OPAL ([OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md)) respectively.
- **String vs perturbative discriminants:** r vs hadron mass and p_out as a function of energy; double-inclusive r₂/r₁ ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)); 1/N_c² azimuthal asymmetries (≲ 2 % in ISR-type events, dominant for quasi-collinear qq̄) and tagged-q-jet tests ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
- Soft-hadron azimuthal asymmetries in γ+jet, W+jet, gq→gq ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)); W+jet done by D0 ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)). Particle flow in qg→qg vs W→qq̄ ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md)).
- Colour-field topology probes: K K̄ correlations, low-momentum correlations in high-p⊥ events, associated-hadron asymmetry in prompt-photon events ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)).

**Colour reconnection**
- WW: |y| < 2 multiplicity at small q–Q̄′ angle and √s/angle scans of the recoupled fraction ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md)) — the √s lever arm is weak because the W width keeps the vertex separation nearly constant ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). Probability of **zero particles in |y| < 0.5** (T > 0.76): 55× higher for recoupled events, 13 signal vs 1.9 background in 5000 hadronic WW events at 10 % recoupling, generator level ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). qqqq vs lνqq, a high-statistics ZZ run with m_Z as calibration, Bose–Einstein correlations vs topology, self-screening m_W methods ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)).
- Single-system CR at the Z: colour-singlet gg pairs in Z → qq̄gg (probability 1/8) hadronizing as closed strings ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)); the resulting gluon-jet rapidity-gap tests were done at LEP and ruled out ARIADNE-1 and default GAL ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)). Rapidity gaps at the Z for area-law CR ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)); e⁺e⁻ gap search proposal of Ellis–Ross (lead in [Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)).
- tt̄: multiplicity and ΣpT outside b/W jets, ⟨pT⟩(n_ch), UE density, cone multiplicity around the top ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)); 1-string vs 3-string tt̄ and b-jet azimuthal asymmetry ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)).
- Heavy-flavour baryon enhancement and future e⁺e⁻ colliders ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)).

**Colour-flow observables**
- Jet pull with hadronic W in tt̄ as a colour-singlet calibration ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md)); done by ATLAS ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)).
- Queued: jet colour ring, H→bb̄ and VBF colour-flow radiation patterns, ZZ→bb̄cc̄ CR test, in-jet Λ_c/D⁰, rope/shoving signatures in small systems.
