## RQ1 — Physical origin of collective colour effects

Two mechanisms recur throughout. The literature itself stresses that at leading colour they make the same predictions, so most data cannot separate them. A third strand, colour correlations among many overlapping strings (MPI, small systems), has grown out of the second and is treated in §3.

### 1. Perturbative soft-gluon coherence (interference)

- **Origin.** Soft, wide-angle gluons cannot resolve the individual colour charges inside a jet; they are emitted coherently by the jet's total colour charge. Ermolaev–Fadin derived, in the double-log approximation (DLA), that the exclusive e⁺e⁻ → qq̄ + n g cross section factorises into a tree of eikonal emissions, each gluon emitting at successively smaller angles, with Casimir C_F or C_A per emitter. They stress that this holds only after *summing* diagrams ("a gauge in which only one diagram contributes does not exist"), so angular ordering is an interference result ([Ermolaev_1981cm](summaries/Ermolaev_1981cm.md)). Bassetto–Ciafaloni–Marchesini give the colour-operator form: the eikonal current J^μ = Σ T_i(…) acts in colour space, large-angle emission "measures the total colour charge" T_Jet, and crossed (interference) diagrams cancel the non-angular-ordered region order by order ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). Mueller 1981 is the parallel original derivation (queued, not read). The letter by Ermolaev–Fadin states its results without derivation, so the non-overlap and completeness of its regions are asserted, not shown ([Ermolaev_1981cm](summaries/Ermolaev_1981cm.md)).
- **Intra-jet consequences (RQ1a).** Angular ordering reduces the multiplicity anomalous dimension by 1/√2 relative to an incoherent ladder, giving n_jet ∝ (log Q²/Λ²)^{K_1} exp(K_0√log(Q²/Λ²)) with K_0 ≈ 1.7, K_1 ≈ 0.73, and a "hump-backed" Gaussian in ln(1/x) peaking at x ≈ (Q_0/Q)^{1/2}; a coherent MC shows the hump, a non-coherent one peaks at x ≈ Q_0/Q instead ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). The same picture underlies MLLA ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)) and the coherent showers ([Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)).
- **Inter-jet coherence (RQ1b, 1d, 1e) needs more than DLA.** DLA keeps only Casimir factors, so colour correlations between emitters, i.e. inter-jet interference, are absent at that accuracy ([Ermolaev_1981cm](summaries/Ermolaev_1981cm.md)); BCM find jets in different angular regions uncorrelated to double-log accuracy, with only single-log inter-jet correlations ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). The inter-jet pattern comes from the soft-gluon *antenna*: for qq̄g, W = N_c(H²₊₁ + H²₋₁) + (2C_F − N_c)H²₊₋, where the q–q̄ term carries a negative coefficient −1/N_c ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)). Midway between q and q̄ the qq̄g/qq̄γ density ratio is 7/16 ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)). BCM's closed-chain formula for multi-gluon emission from a colour singlet (nearest-neighbour 1/(ζ_{i1i2}ζ_{i2i3}⋯) products) is the conceptual root of dipole/antenna emission, though its colour exactness beyond two gluons is not demonstrated in the review ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). In hadron collisions the same decomposition links incoming and outgoing partons ([Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md), [Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md)). Two-gluon azimuthal correlations inside a jet (back-to-back emission suppressed by 7/16 at LO) have "the same dynamical origin as the string effect" according to OPAL ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)).

### 2. Non-perturbative colour connection at hadronization

- **Preconfinement.** In the angular-ordered, planar (large-N_c) cascade, colour-connected partons have Sudakov-damped mass spectra and form colour-singlet clusters ([Amati_1979fg](summaries/Amati_1979fg.md), [Bassetto_1979vy](summaries/Bassetto_1979vy.md), [Marchesini_1980cr](summaries/Marchesini_1980cr.md)). BCM's MC gives general clusters of a few Q_0, but the perturbative colourless qq̄ clusters are "rather large objects" (⟨M⟩ ≈ 10 GeV estimate), which they propose to cure by enhancing g → qq̄, anticipating forced splitting in cluster models ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)).
- **Lund string.** The colour field is stretched q → g → q̄; the gluon is a kink. The two string pieces fragment in their own rest frames and are boosted, so hadrons lie along two hyperbolae and the q–q̄ region is depleted: the **string effect** ([Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md), [Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)). The force on the kink is twice that on a quark, compared by the authors with the QCD ratio 2/(1−1/N²) = 9/4 (= C_A/C_F, our remark) ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)); our reading is that the string thus already encodes the colour-charge ratio driving the antenna pattern ([Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md)). An alternative topology (Montvay octet string + junction) interpolates between string and independent fragmentation; the Lund authors say data favour the kink or a large octet tension ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)).
- **Colour-topology ambiguity → colour reconnection.** Andersson et al. note already in 1983 that the field topology is not fixed by the charges (two ways in qq̄gg, six in gg → gg with octet remnants) and that separating amplitudes by colour configuration is unique only for N_c → ∞ ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)). **CR** asks whether the leading-colour assignment is rearranged before hadronization: between the two W's at LEP2 ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), [Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)), within a single colour singlet such as Z → qq̄g, where a reconnection can leave the gluon as an isolated closed string ([OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md), [ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md)), between the hard system and the proton remnant in DIS ([Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md)), between MPI systems at hadron colliders, or with top-decay products ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md), [Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md), [Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)). With MPIs the ambiguity is large even at N_c → ∞: n gluons kicked out of a beam give n! planar orderings, and these correlations arise below the shower cut-off Q₀ ~ 1 GeV, so perturbation theory does not fix them (p. 40) ([Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md)).
- **Soft colour interactions (SCI) and rapidity gaps.** Edin–Ingelman–Rathsman let every pair of colour charges, proton-remnant spectators included, exchange colour with probability R after the perturbative phase, without changing momenta. Colour-singlet systems separated in rapidity then appear, and DIS rapidity gaps arise without a pomeron; with R = 0.2 the gap events show diffractive-like features (exponential t, ~1/M_X², forward nucleon) without Regge input (pp. 2–5) ([Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md)). This is the HERA-side origin of the idea that soft colour rearrangement acts even within a single colour-singlet system, and the direct ancestor of Rathsman's GAL model ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)). Our assessment: the gap rate depends at least as much on the tuned perturbative ingredients (matrix-element y_cut, shower Q₀²) as on R, so it is not a clean measurement of a reconnection probability ([Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md)). Whether the same strength applies in e⁺e⁻ is contested (RQ3, LEP-1 gluon jets).

### Key structural point: string and antenna coincide at leading colour

Dropping the O(1/N_c²) term, the qq̄g antenna becomes two incoherent qq̄-like dipoles (q–g, g–q̄), each boosted from its rest frame: "3 = 2 + 2 + Lorentz boosts", literally the Lund string mechanism. The authors conclude the two pictures "appear to be rather close" and that the string "catches quite well" the coherent perturbative physics ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)); Khoze et al. later call this "peaceful coexistence" ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). They differ only in (i) the energy and hadron-mass/p_out dependence (perturbative r tends to an energy-independent asymptote for all hadrons; the string boost gives a fixed-scale mass dependence) and (ii) the colour-suppressed negative q–q̄ term, present in pQCD but in neither the string nor leading-colour dipole showers ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md), [Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). These 1/N_c² effects were unobserved as of 1996 ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). The same logic applies to CR: in WW the perturbative reconnection is O(α_s²), colour-suppressed by 1/(N_c²−1) and cut off at ω ≲ Γ_W, and also contains negative-sign "dipoles" with no probabilistic counterpart ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). CR models are therefore attempts to model subleading-colour freedom non-perturbatively: by space-time string overlap ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)), by string-length (λ) minimisation ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md), [Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md), [Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md), [Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md), [Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)), by SU(3) algebra plus string-length minimisation ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)), by cluster-mass reduction ([Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)), or by transverse-space proximity ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md), [Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)).

**A perturbative route to CR.** Gieseke et al. evolve the cluster colour state with the one-loop soft anomalous dimension from the cluster mass M_ij² = 2p_i·p_j down to a scale μ below it; the reconnection probability is the normalised overlap |⟨σ|U|τ⟩|² / Σ_ρ|⟨ρ|U|τ⟩|² (Eqs. 4.2–4.4) ([Gieseke_2018gff](summaries/Gieseke_2018gff.md)). The evolution on its own prefers lower cluster masses and smaller q–q̄ rapidity separation, which the authors read as "strong support for geometric models"; in the two-cluster case p_rec = 1/10 at minimal and 9/10 at maximal mixing (Eq. 5.6), i.e. 1/(N²+1) and N²/(N²+1) (our arithmetic), and baryonic reconnections (~2–12 %, Fig. 6) emerge naturally. They argue CR is "not simply a 1/N suppressed effect" (p. 18). Our assessment: the evolution is extrapolated below the shower cut-off (μ down to 0.01 GeV), α_s and μ act as tuning parameters, the studies are toy cluster ensembles without data, and the 1/N claim rests on transposition counts, not a 1/N expansion; so this is a perturbatively inspired model, not a derivation ([Gieseke_2018gff](summaries/Gieseke_2018gff.md)). It does, however, give the first concrete link between CR and the beyond-leading-colour soft evolution behind §1.

### 3. Colour correlations among many strings: MPI, ropes, junctions, geometry (small systems)

At hadron colliders many strings are produced in a small transverse area. Several mechanisms then compete as the origin of "collective" behaviour without a plasma; the scope notes of the review make this part of RQ1.

- **⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) as the primary hadron-collider argument for colour correlations.** If each extra MPI adds its own strings, multiplicity grows with the number of MPIs while ⟨p⊥⟩ stays roughly constant; a rising ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) requires that extra MPIs add proportionally less to n_ch than to Σp⊥, which "strongly depends on the colour connections between the interactions" (p. 11) ([Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md)). Sjöstrand–Skands find that initial-state colour orderings alone (random, rapidity- or λ-ordered) stay nearly flat at ≈ 0.41–0.43 GeV out to N_ch = 150 against ≈ 0.56 GeV for Tune A (read off Fig. 20); they conclude that the colour flow "cannot be correctly described by merely arranging the colour lines present in the initial state" (p. 46) and that "the colours of final-state partons are not random but correlated, somehow, to give a reduced string length" (p. 60). Their crude final-state CR reaches the Tune A slope, but they stress it is "not … evidence for the existence of colour reconnections" (p. 48) ([Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md)). Skands–Wicke find the same at the Tevatron: their no-CR model rises only from ≈ 0.4 to ≈ 0.45 GeV between N_ch ≈ 40 and 140, against ≈ 0.55–0.62 GeV for all models with colour correlations (read off Fig. 1) ([Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)). Pythia 8 without CR is ≈ 20–25 % too low at high n_ch against CDF and ATLAS data (read off Fig. 1) ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)), and Sjöstrand's 2013 review states that CR is required for ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) ([Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md)). Our assessment: the 2004 and 2007 papers compare with Tune A, not with data, and Tune A itself contains ad hoc colour correlations; the argument shows that *some* final-state colour correlation reducing string length is needed within the MPI + string framework, not that CR is the unique explanation ([Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md), [Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)).
- **CR as a source of radial-flow-like effects.** In Pythia 8 (Tune 4C, MPI-based CR), string pieces joining partons from different MPIs get a common transverse boost, giving a hadron-mass-ordered hardening: with CR, proton ⟨p_T⟩ rises from ≈ 0.67 to ≈ 1.2 GeV/c over the multiplicity range, without CR only from ≈ 0.63 to ≈ 0.75 (read off Fig. 4); the p/π bump at p_T ≈ 2.5–3 GeV/c grows for ≥ 20 MPIs and vanishes for < 5 MPIs and with CR off (Fig. 3) ([OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg](summaries/OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg.md)). The authors say the effect fades at high p_T, unlike hydrodynamics, and call CR-generated v₂/v₃ "very speculative" (p. 4). Our assessment: the evidence is MC-vs-MC within one tune; data appear only in one ALICE preliminary p/π comparison, and the blast-wave and CMS ⟨p_T⟩ claims are made in the text without plots ([OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg](summaries/OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg.md)). The idea is also cited by Sjöstrand ([Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md)).
- **Ropes.** Strings overlapping in transverse space (r₀ ≈ 1 fm) and rapidity add their colour charges as a random walk in SU(3) to a multiplet {p,q}; each break-up then sees an effective tension equal to the drop in Casimir, κ̃/κ = (2p+q+2)/4 (Eq. 3.4), much smaller than the Schwinger-type (2p−1)κ ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)). A higher κ̃ enhances strangeness and baryons; the typical pp enhancement is ⟨κ̃/κ⟩ ≈ 1.16–1.22 at LHC energies (read off Fig. 12). Colour singlets from 3⊗3̄ are handled by a final-state "swing", itself a λ-minimising CR in the ARIADNE lineage, and the authors attribute part of the flow-like low-p⊥ rise of p/π and Λ/K to that CR and the rest to rope tension (pp. 25–29) ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)).
- **String junctions.** CR with SU(3) colour rules can join three string pieces at a junction, which hadronizes into an extra baryon ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)). Junctions also carry the beam baryon number in the Pythia MPI framework and can move it to central rapidity ("baryon stopping"); junction baryons can carry |S| = 3 (Fig. 27) ([Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md)). In the current Pythia QCD-CR model, junction baryons make up to ≈ 50 % of all baryons (peaking near p⊥ ≈ 3 GeV) and up to ≈ 75 % of heavy-flavour baryons at low p⊥ at 7 TeV (Fig. 13); this is the model's explanation of the LHC Λc⁺/D⁰ rise ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)).
- **Geometry and causality.** Two recent models restrict CR to partons close in transverse space. Herwig 7 spacetime CR smears final partons around their MPI centres and accepts reconnections that reduce ΣR with R_ij² = Δr_ij²/d₀² + Δy_ij²; the tune gives d₀ = 0.15 fm (Tab. 1) ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)). Pythia/Angantyr forbids QCD-CR between dipoles more than δb_c apart in impact parameter; this allows the first CR across nucleon–nucleon sub-collisions in pA/AA, and near-global CR (δb_c = 7 fm) lowers mean multiplicity by ≈ 10 % in PbPb (Fig. 10) ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)). Our assessment: in both, the length scale is a tuning compromise (δb_c = 0.5 fm balances pp against pA; d₀ is well defined in the χ² but the companion ν² is not), only transverse positions are used, so the causality motivation is not yet implemented, and neither computes flow observables ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md), [Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)).

  The authors of the junction and rope papers read the mechanisms as complementary: junctions/CR for baryons, a separate strangeness mechanism (e.g. ropes) for Ξ/Ω ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md), [Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)). No combined fit exists in the papers read, and the "CR vs rope" split of the flow-like signal is open ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)).

### Colour entanglement

A further idea is soft colour exchange with spectators breaking TMD factorisation in hadron collisions (RQ1e). A PHENIX search found no clear signal; an apparent width decrease at 510 GeV was a selection bias, and properly binned widths follow standard (CSS) evolution ([Osborn_2018bwn](summaries/Osborn_2018bwn.md)).

## RQ2 — Theoretical results and models

### Coherence: analytic results and coherent showers
- **DLA/LLA foundations:** coherent branching with C_F/C_A per emitter and an angular-ordered Sudakov form factor ([Ermolaev_1981cm](summaries/Ermolaev_1981cm.md)); colour-operator factorisation, angular-ordered generating functional, multiplicity and hump-backed plateau, KNO dispersions (c_2 ≈ 1/3 gluon jet, ≈ 3/4 quark jet), and an O(√α_s) subleading shift of the peak toward small x that "may contribute … to make the experimental detection of the dip more difficult" ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)).
- **MLLA + LPHD:** antenna patterns and the hump-backed plateau ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)); MLLA predicts a ξ-peak slope b = 0.627 vs ln E_cm ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md)). LPHD is called the "Achilles heel": not derivable from QCD ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
- **Coherent showers:** angular-ordered final-state showers with planar colour flow and cluster hadronization ([Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)); extension to initial–final colour connections, dropping 1/N² terms ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md), [Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md)); colour-dipole cascade (ARIADNE), equivalent to a string with gluon kinks and leading-colour by construction ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md), [Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)). Azimov et al. argued in 1985 that the angular-ordered Marchesini–Webber MC "oversimplifies" inter-jet coherence ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)); JADE nevertheless found it reproduces the string effect (RQ3).

### The string effect: competing predictions
- **Lund (1980):** an event-level correlation of the gluon-jet multiplicity excess and the angular asymmetry, predicted "of the order of 16%"; at √s = 30 GeV (MC) Δn·Δθ > 0 in 60 % of target events (58 % with backgrounds) vs 50 % without the effect. This is a string-vs-independent-fragmentation test with no perturbative argument ([Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md)).
- **Perturbative antenna (1985):** for the JADE/TPC geometry, r = N₁₃/N₁₂ = 2.56 if jet 3 is always the gluon, 1.17 for an incoherent model that keeps the gluon's larger charge, 1.62 for the realistic gluon-assignment mixture; with the three-jet selection, r_PT = 1.50 (JADE) and 1.55 (TPC). Hence the effect comes from interference, not gluon dominance ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)).
- **Discriminating the two (1990):** ARIADNE + JETSET vs JETSET string only, symmetric 120° qq̄g. With the cascade r₁ falls with energy (2.74 at 30 GeV → 2.38 at 93 GeV → 2.13 at 750 GeV); string-only gives 2.69 → 2.99 → 2.89. The hadron-mass and p_out dependence of r is "drastically reduced already for LEP energies" with the cascade. The double-inclusive r₂ = ⟨N₂N₃⟩/⟨N₁N₃⟩ is below r₁ with the cascade (2.52 vs 2.74 at 30 GeV) but equal for string only ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)). Our assessment: these are MC-only statements, the two setups use different Lund parameters, and since ARIADNE drops the 1/N_c² term its r₂ < r₁ comes from perturbative spill-over, so the comparison is "string topology with vs without a cascade", not "string vs full pQCD" ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)).
- **Colour flow and "QCD measurements":** the colour-flow assumption changed CELLO's α_s from ≈ 0.17 (independent fragmentation) to ≈ 0.25 (Lund); the Lund authors concluded that separating perturbative QCD and fragmentation "is lost" at PETRA energies ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)).

### Colour-reconnection models I: the e⁺e⁻ / DIS era (1988–1999)
- **Gustafson–Pettersson–Zerwas 1988 (origin).** W decay vertices ~0.1 fm apart ≪ 1 fm, so cross pairings qQ̄′, Qq̄′ can form singlets; naive colour counting gives 1/9 = 1/N_c². Type-I (bag-like) vacuum would enhance, type-II (thin vortex) suppress recoupling. As a 100 %-recoupled toy without gluon radiation before recoupling, multiplicity at α = 30° falls from 45.0 to 22.7 and the central region empties. The paper already warns that W-property measurements must consider this ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md)).
- **Sjöstrand–Khoze 1994 (SK-I/II/II′).** Perturbative CR is negligible: ΔN/N ≲ O(10⁻²), m_W effect ≲ 5 MeV. Non-perturbative CR via space-time string overlap: SK-I (Gaussian flux tubes, free k_I), SK-II (vortex lines, reconnect at first core crossing, no free strength), SK-II′ (only if string length decreases). With k_I = 0.6, "a realistic scenario with ~30 % reconnection" at 170 GeV; m_W shifts at 170 GeV of +14 to −10 MeV (I), −21 to −34 MeV (II), −17 to −31 MeV (II′); toy extremes up to +576 MeV. Best estimate ~40 MeV total CR uncertainty. Inclusive observables are nearly blind (⟨n_ch⟩ 36.63 → 36.31/36.45) ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)).
- **Gustafson–Häkkinen 1994.** Gluons with E ≳ Γ_W are radiated by each W before recoupling, so two parton chains (⟨n⟩ ≈ 4.3 gluons each) offer (n+1)(m+1) recoupling options; group theory only bounds the probability to 0.03 < P < 0.97, so 1/9 is "not a derived probability". Proposed dynamics: recouple to the configuration minimising string length λ, benchmark rate ~10 % (an input), ⟨Δλ⟩ ≈ 2 vs ≈ 0.7 for SK-I. They regard SK-I's strength as having "no dynamical motivation" ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)).
- **Edin–Ingelman–Rathsman 1995 (SCI).** Colour exchange between any pair of colour charges, including remnant spectators, with fixed probability R (default 0.2, motivated as α_s(0.5 GeV)/π), momenta unchanged; implemented in LEPTO 6.3. The gap rate saturates above R ≈ 0.5 (p. 3) ([Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md)).
- **Lönnblad 1996 (ARIADNE CR).** Random colour index in [1,9] per dipole mimics 1/N_c²; equal-index dipoles swap if λ decreases, iterated after every emission (so partly perturbative). For WW, cross-talk only for E_g < Γ_W (the AR-2 prescription per DELPHI). m_W shift ≈ +32 MeV at 170 GeV (range +23 to +58 MeV, threshold-dependent), with MC-truth jet assignment. Intra-system CR at the Z is retunable but hardens p_⊥out. CR in the unordered dipole cascade gives far too few HERA rapidity-gap events and the wrong m_X shape ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)).
- **Rathsman 1999 (generalised area law, GAL).** SCI with the constant probability replaced by P = R₀[1 − exp(−bΔA)] in the decrease of Lund string area; R₀ = 0.1 fixed by HERA diffractive DIS; predicts up to 2× more large-gap Z events and Δm_W ≈ +65 MeV ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)). LEP-1 later wanted R₀ ≈ 0.01–0.02 (RQ3) ([ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md)).
- **Our reading of the lineage.** Two families exist from 1994 on: space-time overlap (SK) and momentum-space λ-minimisation (GH → ARIADNE → GAL → Pythia QCD-based/gluon-move style) ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md), [Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)), with SCI → GAL as a parallel "soft colour exchange" branch motivated by DIS gaps ([Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md), [Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)). λ-minimising models can only shorten strings and so only reduce multiplicity; SK-I allows ~20 % length-increasing crossings ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md), [Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). The modern spacetime models (below) partly revive the SK idea in hadron collisions.

### Colour-reconnection models II: hadron colliders (2004–2024)
- **MPI colour flow and the first final-state CR (2004).** In the interleaved Pythia 6.3 MPI framework the beam baryon number is carried by a junction; three initial-state colour orderings fail ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch), and a crude final-state CR swapping colour tags when λ decreases, with strength F, reaches it (F = 1 overshoots Tune A, Fig. 21). It is applied only to events with ≥ 2 interactions, "to avoid … large central rapidity gaps" ([Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md)).
- **Colour annealing (2007).** Each string piece survives with probability (1 − ξ_R)^{n_int} (Eq. 1); the rest are re-strung by minimising Λ = Π m_i²/M₀² (Eq. 2); types 0/1/2 differ in gluon-loop suppression; top and W decay products take part ([Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)). Our note: the (1 − ξ_R)^{n_int} scaling is explicitly a rough ansatz, and no LEP constraint is applied, on the grounds that jet universality may be violated ([Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)).
- **QCD-based CR, gluon-move, Herwig cluster CR (2012–2015).** Pythia QCD-based CR (SU(3) colour rules, causality, junctions) fits LEP, LHC ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) and Λ/K⁰_S; relative to the old CR it raises Λc⁺ and Λb⁰ by about ×2 and Σc, Σb by about ×20–30; it fails identified-hadron p⊥ spectra and rapidity gaps ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)). CR is required for ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch), may mimic flow ([Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md)). Herwig++ cluster CR restores ATLAS ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) and dN/dη but fails diffraction-enhanced high-n_ch minimum bias ([Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)). Gluon-move (GM) minimises λ = Σ ln(1 + m_ij²/m₀²) by gluon moves and string flips, with no colour algebra or space-time ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)).
- **Ropes (2015).** Rope hadronization in DIPSY + ARIADNE + Pythia 8, with κ̃/κ from Casimir differences; flavour parameters rescale as ρ → ρ^{1/h} etc. (Eqs. 2.8–2.9); new parameters r₀ = 1 fm, β = 0.25, m₀ = 0.135 GeV are set to physical estimates, not tuned; for AA the authors predict a 15–20 % multiplicity decrease without retuning (p. 10) ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)).
- **CR from soft-gluon evolution (2018).** See RQ1 key structural point. Proposed model inputs: the analytic two-cluster p_rec(M_i/M_f) instead of a flat probability, a dynamic baryonic-CR probability, and "unbaryonization"; announced as ongoing work, apparently not in Herwig by late 2019 ([Gieseke_2018gff](summaries/Gieseke_2018gff.md), [Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)).
- **Herwig 7 spacetime CR (2019).** Tune ν² = 4.5 GeV², d₀ = 0.15 fm, baryonic weight w_b = 0.98 with retuned MPI (Tab. 1). "Reasonable agreement" for 7 TeV η and p⊥ spectra, but the N_ch distribution overshoots at high N_ch (MC/data reaching ≈ 1.3–1.5, read off Fig. 10), Σp⊥ in the UE is ≈ 10 % too large, and protons are "fairly poorly described" ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)). Our assessment: the data used are largely insensitive to the new spacetime parameters, and no default-tune curves are shown, so the paper shows comparable, not better, agreement than momentum-space CR ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)).
- **Spatially constrained QCD-CR in Angantyr (2023).** `ColourReconnection:dipoleMaxDist` (δb_c). In pp, reducing δb_c weakens CR, raises multiplicity and flattens ⟨p⊥⟩(N_ch); the retune (δb_c = 0.5 fm, m₀ 2.9 → 1.05 GeV, Tab. 3) restores ⟨N_ch⟩ and ⟨p⊥⟩(N_ch) but the high-N_ch tail drops too fast and the p⊥ spectrum becomes too hard (Fig. 12). The authors call the model a "first attempt" and "quite naive" (p. 20) ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)).
- **String junctions revisited (2024).** A new junction-rest-frame algorithm (the old one failed for ≈ 10 % of LHC min-bias events), a "pearl on a string" treatment of massive slow legs, and a mass-aware λ measure (default from Pythia 8.311). Global pp observables are unchanged within ≈ 10 %, and the junction-baryon fractions are essentially unchanged; physics conclusions come from the junction mechanism itself, not from the revision (our assessment) ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)).

### CR and the top-quark mass
- **Tevatron toy (2007).** Generator-level semileptonic tt̄ with truth-matched jets: tunes differ by about ±1.1 GeV (fitted) and ±1.5 GeV (JES-rescaled) in calibration offset; the authors split this into ~±1 GeV perturbative (virtuality- vs p⊥-ordered shower) and ~±0.5 GeV "non-perturbative" spread within a shower class, which includes CR (p. 6) ([Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)). Our assessment: the ±0.5 GeV is a spread of tunes that also differ in UE, beam-remnant and IR parameters, not an isolated CR on/off effect ([Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)).
- **Pythia 8 era.** CR on/off shifts m_t by about 0.15 GeV in Pythia 8.1 ([Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md)). Top-specific CR: tuned m_top shifts −0.6 to +0.36 GeV, about +200/−800 MeV around default for all models and ~500 MeV realistic for the minimisation models ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)); QCD-based CR calibrated on m_W gives smaller shifts ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)). The ATLAS tt̄ paper quotes a CR uncertainty on m_top of up to 400 MeV from a CMS reference (p. 4) ([ATLAS_2022ctr](summaries/ATLAS_2022ctr.md)).
- **Our reading.** The "~0.5 GeV" number has persisted from a 2007 tune-spread toy to 2014 model variations; neither is a measurement, and the one dedicated tt̄ CR measurement so far constrains MPI-level CR more than top-decay CR (RQ3) ([Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md), [Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md), [ATLAS_2022ctr](summaries/ATLAS_2022ctr.md)).

### CR predictions for future e⁺e⁻ colliders
Christiansen–Sjöstrand re-run LEP2 WW → 4q with the LHC-era Pythia 8 models ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)):
- **The sign of δm_W is model-dependent.** At 170 GeV (Tab. 1, pairing-method range): SK-I +10 to +18 MeV, SK-II −13 to −18 MeV, GM-I −27 to −41 MeV, GM-II +31 to +49 MeV, GM-III and CS within ±7 MeV (in GM-III the move and flip steps cancel almost exactly, which the authors call accidental). SK-I and SK-II differ from the original SK numbers ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)) because the shower is now p⊥-ordered. Our assessment, following the summary: a single "CR on/off" systematic is therefore not conservative.
- **Energy dependence.** Shifts grow at 240 GeV (GM-II ≈ +370 to +400 MeV, GM-III ≈ +100 MeV, SK-I ≈ +90 MeV) and fall again at 350 GeV; CS shows almost no effect at any energy (Tab. 2).
- **The tt̄ threshold** (three colour singlets W, W, bb̄) offers more CR possibilities; only early studies exist (p. 10).

### Colour-flow observables
Jet pull (pull angle points to the colour partner; ~1.5× signal/background shape difference in simulated ZH→bb̄) ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md)); dipolarity for top tagging, generator-dependent gains ([Hook_2011cq](summaries/Hook_2011cq.md)).

**How the LEP2 outcome compares with the 1988–96 theory.**
- The qualitative SK prediction held: CR small in inclusive observables but a leading m_W(qqqq) systematic. SK's ~40 MeV estimate is close to the final 35 MeV, but our assessment is that the match is partly coincidental: LEP obtained 35 MeV only after soft-particle de-weighting, with k_I at its 68 % CL upper limit (2.10), and found shifts up to ~100 MeV with standard reconstruction ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)). OPAL confirms the inclusive blindness directly: even 100 % SK-I shifts ⟨n^4q⟩ by only ~1 % against a 0.7 % error ([OPAL_2005cdr](summaries/OPAL_2005cdr.md)).
- LEP prefers roughly twice SK's reference strength (~51 % at 189 GeV vs ~30 % at 170 GeV), inside the factor-2 model uncertainty SK allowed; k_I values transfer only within the same PYTHIA implementation ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). OPAL's own SK-I fit (37 %, 10–56 % at 68 % CL) lies between L3 and DELPHI and is compatible with the LEP average; the OPAL p_reco = 0.43 listed in the LEP report comes from the OPAL m_W paper (particle flow combined with ΔM_W), not from the particle-flow paper ([OPAL_2005cdr](summaries/OPAL_2005cdr.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)). All are far above the 1/9 colour-counting baseline of [Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), consistent with GH's point that 1/9 does not fix the rate ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)).
- The striking event-by-event signals of the 1988 toy (multiplicity halved, empty centre) were revised away by the authors once hard-gluon emission before recoupling was included ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), [Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). The particle-flow ratio R_N that LEP did use descends from the inter-W depletion idea ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)); the m_W-estimator method is close to SK's "self-screening" suggestion ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)).
- ARIADNE's +32 MeV (MC-truth reconstruction) shrank to ΔM_W(std, cone) = 7.2 ± 4.1 MeV in DELPHI's realistic analysis, as Lönnblad expected ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)). ALEPH infers from the Z gluon-jet test that, if Z and WW reconnection are alike, JETSET+GAL and ARIADNE CR overestimate the CR-induced m_W shift (p. 17) ([ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md)); our note: this would apply to GAL's predicted +65 MeV ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)), not to SK-I, which the Z test cannot probe.
- With LHC-era models the LEP2 data remain weakly constraining: of the Pythia 8 models tested (SK-I/II/II′, GM-I/II/III, CS and two maximal variants) only GM-I is excluded by r, while the models' m_W shifts span both signs ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)).

**Emerging pattern.**
1. Coherence-free models fail in every collider environment. In e⁺e⁻ the most convincing evidence comes from angular correlations ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md), [L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md)) and the string effect vs incoherent showers ([JADE_1985mmd](summaries/JADE_1985mmd.md)); the ξ spectrum, long cited as the flagship test, discriminates only weakly once string hadronization is retuned ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md), [Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
2. Whether inter-jet colour effects are "perturbative" or "string" remains experimentally open: the two coincide at leading colour, and the proposed discriminants (energy/mass dependence, r₂/r₁, 1/N_c² asymmetries) have not been seen to settle it ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md), [Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
3. At hadron colliders the best generators still miss precise unfolded data (CMS forward region), so "coherence is understood" is overstated.
4. CR between colour singlets is favoured at LEP2 (2.8σ, SK-I-specific; OPAL alone ~1.2–1.4σ by our reading) and needed in the MPI + string picture for ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) and for the ATLAS tt̄ UE observables, but no observable isolates it model-independently, and at the LHC it is degenerate with MPI tuning ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md), [OPAL_2005cdr](summaries/OPAL_2005cdr.md), [ATLAS_2022ctr](summaries/ATLAS_2022ctr.md)). Its main practical impact is as a systematic on m_W and m_top, where even the sign of the m_W shift is model-dependent ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)).
5. Our reading: the soft-colour-rearrangement strength is not universal across processes in the models tested. Hadron-initiated processes appear to want sizeable rearrangement (HERA gaps with SCI R = 0.2 or GAL R₀ = 0.1; ~50 % reconnection at LEP2; strong CR in pp), whereas inside a single e⁺e⁻ colour singlet the LEP-1 gluon-jet gap data want R₀ ≈ 0.01–0.02 or consistent with zero ([Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md), [Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md), [ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md), [OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md)). OPAL suggests the hadron remnant may supply a mechanism absent in e⁺e⁻ ([OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md)); different shower cut-offs may also absorb part of it ([ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md)).
6. In small systems no single colour mechanism describes baryon and strangeness production: junction CR gets Λ and Λc but overshoots Λb⁰/B⁰ (and p/π in one tune) and misses Ξ, Ω, Ξc; ropes get strangeness but not the high-p⊥ fall; CR gives radial-flow-like mass ordering in MC only ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md), [Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md), [Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md), [OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg](summaries/OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg.md)).

## RQ4 — Experimental tests proposed in the theory literature

**Intra-jet coherence**
- Multiplicity growth, small-x depletion (look for depletion rather than the dip, which hadronization may fill), KNO dispersions, single/double-cone energy-flow form factors; also "higher energy p–p̄ collider jets" ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)).
- Energy dependence of the hump-backed peak in a restricted cone, kaons and baryons first ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)). Peak evolution done by OPAL + TASSO ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md)); the flavour dependence of identified-particle ξ spectra, absent in MLLA, is suggested as a further check by [Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md).
- EMMC (Dokshitzer et al.) and two-particle azimuthal correlations: done by OPAL ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)).

**Inter-jet coherence and the string effect**
- Lund gluon-tagging asymmetry Δn·Δθ ([Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md)); superseded in practice by the inter-jet population ratio ([JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md)).
- qq̄g vs qq̄γ (the negative "third antenna") and the string effect with heavy-quark-tagged quark jets ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)); done by DELPHI ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)) and OPAL ([OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md)) respectively.
- **String vs perturbative discriminants:** r vs hadron mass and p_out as a function of energy; double-inclusive r₂/r₁ ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)); 1/N_c² azimuthal asymmetries (≲ 2 % in ISR-type events, dominant for quasi-collinear qq̄) and tagged-q-jet tests ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
- Soft-hadron azimuthal asymmetries in γ+jet, W+jet, gq→gq ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)); W+jet done by D0 ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)). Particle flow in qg→qg vs W→qq̄ ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md)).
- Colour-field topology probes: K K̄ correlations, low-momentum correlations in high-p⊥ events, associated-hadron asymmetry in prompt-photon events ([Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)).

**Colour reconnection in e⁺e⁻**
- WW: |y| < 2 multiplicity at small q–Q̄′ angle and √s/angle scans of the recoupled fraction ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md)) — the √s lever arm is weak because the W width keeps the vertex separation nearly constant ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). Probability of **zero particles in |y| < 0.5** (T > 0.76): 55× higher for recoupled events, 13 signal vs 1.9 background in 5000 hadronic WW events at 10 % recoupling, generator level ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). qqqq vs lνqq, a high-statistics ZZ run with m_Z as calibration, Bose–Einstein correlations vs topology, self-screening m_W methods ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). The multiplicity comparison qqqq vs lνqq was done by OPAL and found insensitive at LEP precision ([OPAL_2005cdr](summaries/OPAL_2005cdr.md)).
- Single-system CR at the Z: colour-singlet gg pairs in Z → qq̄gg (probability 1/8) hadronizing as closed strings ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)); the gluon-jet rapidity-gap tests (even-n_ch spikes, Q_leading = 0 beyond the gap, proposed via Friberg–Gustafson–Häkkinen and Minkowski–Ochs) were done by OPAL and ALEPH and ruled out ARIADNE-1/Ariadne-CR and default GAL ([OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md), [ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)). Our assessment, following [OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md): this is a sharp, underused test for any modern CR or rope model that acts inside a single system, and a candidate for a Z-pole re-analysis at a future collider. Rapidity gaps at the Z for area-law CR ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)); e⁺e⁻ gap search proposal of Ellis–Ross (lead in [Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)).
- **Future e⁺e⁻ (FCC-ee/CEPC-type):** repeat m_W(4q) − m_W(2qℓν) at 240 GeV, where model shifts reach O(100 MeV); the hadronic/semileptonic multiplicity ratio N_ch(4q)/(N_ch(qqℓν) − 1), exactly 2 without CR and 1.93–1.97 with CR at 170 GeV (no uncertainties quoted); in H → WW → 4q, CR distorts the parity angles about as much as a 2–5 % CP-odd admixture, but shifts Φ opposite to CP-oddness, so a simultaneous fit could separate them; dedicated CR studies at the tt̄ threshold ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)). Revisit e⁺e⁻ → WW for junction–antijunction CR (enhanced back-to-back baryon–antibaryon pairs, not modelled in LEP-era CR); compare triplet vs octet string fragmentation in gluon-jet tips and LEP "hairpin" configurations ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)).

**Colour reconnection and colour correlations in hadron collisions**
- ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) as "a prime test for any physical model of the colour flow"; UE "lumpiness" (n-jet rates vs resolution, as in e⁺e⁻); baryon − antibaryon rapidity distributions (baryon stopping) and junction-baryon strangeness ([Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md)). Constrain the non-perturbative m_top uncertainty with Drell–Yan and dijet UE at Q ~ m_top ([Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)).
- tt̄: multiplicity and ΣpT outside b/W jets, ⟨pT⟩(n_ch), UE density, cone multiplicity around the top ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)); 1-string vs 3-string tt̄ and b-jet azimuthal asymmetry ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). The first of these (n_ch and Σp_T outside jets, also in n_ch bins) was done by ATLAS; the top-decay-specific probes (cone multiplicity around the top vs ΔR(W,b)) have not been located in data ([ATLAS_2022ctr](summaries/ATLAS_2022ctr.md)).
- **CR vs hydrodynamic flow:** mass-ordered ⟨p_T⟩(N_ch), the p/π and Λ/K⁰_S peak vs multiplicity and N_MPI, and the fall-off of the CR on/off effect at high p_T, which the authors say distinguishes CR from hydrodynamics ([OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg](summaries/OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg.md)). Studies of angular flow, fluctuations and correlations in pp/pA/AA to separate plasma, rope tension, CR and rope pressure ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)).
- **Ropes:** rope/no-rope enhancement growing with √s, ≈ 1.29 → 1.45 for p/π, ≈ 1.18 → 1.36 for Ξ⁻/Λ, ≈ 1.38 → 1.40 for Λ/K⁰_S, ≈ 1.11 → 1.17 for K/π from ≈ 0.9 to ≈ 14 TeV (read off Fig. 11), called "rather firm predictions" for Run 2 ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)).
- **Junctions and heavy flavour:** Λb/Λc, B⁰/D⁰ and Ξb ratios, global properties of events with low-p⊥ heavy hadrons, and baryon–antibaryon correlation strength to tell junction baryons (weakly correlated) from diquark breaks (strong) and popcorn (milder); no quantitative predictions given ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)). Heavy-flavour baryon enhancement and future e⁺e⁻ colliders ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)); the LHC Λc⁺/D⁰, Λb⁰/B⁰ and Ξc comparisons in RQ3 now test these ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)).
- **Spacetime:** Bose–Einstein correlations vs hadron emission position as a spacetime-sensitive CR probe; restricting CR to MPI subsystems close in spacetime ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)).

**Soft colour interactions in DIS**
- F₂^D(β, Q²) from the SCI model vs data; the forward-system composition (nucleon vs Δ) and variations of the gap definition, where differences from pomeron models are expected; higher-statistics x, Q² dependence of the gap rate ([Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md)). F₂^D(3) was compared for GAL ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)).

**Colour-flow observables**
- Jet pull with hadronic W in tt̄ as a colour-singlet calibration ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md)); done by ATLAS ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)).
- Queued: jet colour ring, H→bb̄ and VBF colour-flow radiation patterns, ZZ→bb̄cc̄ CR test, in-jet Λ_c/D⁰, string shoving and flow observables in small systems, EIC projections.
