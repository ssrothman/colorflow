# Synthesis: collective colour effects in high-energy QCD

_Status: after iteration 2 (27 papers read, 2026-10-30). Covered so far: founding theory of coherence and preconfinement, the classic and LEP-1 string/drag effect, Tevatron and LHC initial–final coherence, LEP2 colour reconnection in WW (incl. the LEP-wide combination), CR models (Pythia QCD-based, Herwig cluster CR, generalised area law), colour flow in tt̄ and jet-pull observables, and a first look at colour entanglement. Not yet covered: rope/junction models and small-system collectivity (ridge, strangeness enhancement), rapidity gaps / colour-singlet exchange, beyond-leading-colour showers, EIC prospects; and 16 key pre-arXiv papers are blocked pending full text. Claims cite `summaries/<ID>.md`; all relevance-3 summaries are independently verified against the papers._

## RQ1 — Physical origin of collective colour effects

Two distinct mechanisms keep appearing, and the literature itself stresses that they are hard to separate:

1. **Perturbative soft-gluon coherence (interference).** Soft, long-wavelength gluons cannot resolve individual colour charges, so radiation from a parton system is governed by its *colour topology* ("QCD radiophysics") ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md), [Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)).
   - *Intra-jet (RQ1a):* angular ordering → hump-backed plateau, reduced multiplicity growth.
   - *Inter-jet (RQ1b, 1d, 1e):* radiation is a sum of colour-connected "antennae"; regions between colour-*unconnected* partons are depleted (7/16 suppression between q and q̄ in qq̄g vs qq̄γ) ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)). In hadron collisions the same decomposition links incoming and outgoing partons ([Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md), [Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md)).
2. **Non-perturbative colour connection at hadronization.** At leading log and large N_c the cascade is planar: partons form adjacent colour-singlet clusters, power-damped in mass and local in space-time (preconfinement) ([Amati_1979fg](summaries/Amati_1979fg.md), [Bassetto_1979vy](summaries/Bassetto_1979vy.md), [Marchesini_1980cr](summaries/Marchesini_1980cr.md)); strings/clusters hadronize along these connections. **Colour reconnection (CR)** asks whether this assignment is rearranged before hadronization: between the two W's at LEP2, between MPI systems at hadron colliders, or with top-decay products ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md), [Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md), [Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)).

**Key structural point.** At large N_c the perturbative antenna picture and the Lund string *coincide*; the two can only be told apart through colour-suppressed (1/N_c²) effects, which were unobserved as of 1996 ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). So "string effect vs drag effect" is not a sharp experimental question at leading colour. CR models are, in the same sense, attempts to model the *subleading-colour* freedom: the QCD-based Pythia model uses SU(3) colour algebra to decide which non-leading connections (incl. junctions) are allowed, then moves to a local string-length minimum ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)); Herwig reconnects clusters to lower total cluster mass ([Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)); the generalised area law swaps string pieces with probability set by area reduction ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)).

A third, distinct idea is **colour entanglement**: soft colour exchange with spectators breaking TMD factorization in hadron collisions (RQ1e). A dedicated PHENIX search found no clear signal; an apparent width decrease at 510 GeV was a selection bias, and properly binned widths follow standard (CSS) evolution ([Osborn_2018bwn](summaries/Osborn_2018bwn.md)).

## RQ2 — Theoretical results and models

- **Coherent parton showers:** angular-ordered final-state showers with planar colour flow and cluster hadronization ([Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)); extension to incoming partons and initial–final colour connections, dropping non-planar (1/N²) terms ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md), from [Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md)).
- **Analytic coherence:** antenna patterns, MLLA, LPHD ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)); MLLA+LPHD describes the W+jet soft-radiation pattern without any hadronization model ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)).
- **CR models and what they are tuned to:**
  - *Sjöstrand–Khoze SK-I/SK-II* (space-time string overlap): the language of all LEP2 limits ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md), [L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md), [DELPHI_2006tie](summaries/DELPHI_2006tie.md)).
  - *Pythia QCD-based CR:* fits LEP, LHC ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) and Λ/K⁰_S; predicts ~20× more charm/bottom baryons; fails identified-hadron p⊥ spectra, ⟨p⊥⟩ vs hadron mass and rapidity gaps ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)). Pythia's own review: CR is required for the rise of ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch), may mimic flow, and contributes up to ~0.5 GeV to m_top uncertainty ([Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md)).
  - *Herwig++ cluster CR (plain and statistical):* restores ATLAS ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) and dN/dη without changing LEP Z observables, but still fails diffraction-enhanced high-n_ch minimum bias; statistical CR is worse there ([Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)).
  - *Generalised area law:* strength fixed on HERA diffractive DIS; predicts up to 2× more large rapidity gaps at the Z and Δm_W ≈ +65 MeV (prediction only) ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)).
  - *Top-specific CR:* default Pythia CR leaves top-decay products alone; forced/swap/move models give tuned m_top shifts of −0.6 to +0.36 GeV, ~500 MeV realistic uncertainty ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)); QCD-based CR, calibrated on m_W, gives smaller shifts for its own model variants ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)).
- **Colour-flow observables:** jet pull (pull angle points to the colour partner; ~1.5× signal/background shape difference in simulated ZH→bb̄) ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md)); dipolarity for top tagging, with gains that depend on the shower generator ([Hook_2011cq](summaries/Hook_2011cq.md)).

## RQ3 — Experimental results and constraints

| Effect | Key result | Discriminating power |
|---|---|---|
| String effect, e⁺e⁻ → 3 jets | JADE 1981: central-region particle ratio 1.35 ± 0.09 vs Lund 1.34, independent fragmentation 1.08; gluon jet by energy ordering (~50 % correct) ([JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md)). OPAL with lepton-tagged quark jets: R̃ = 1.62 ± 0.07 (8.9σ, stat.); Jetset 1.67, Cojets ≈1.02 ([OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md)). DELPHI qq̄γ/qq̄g ratio R_γ = 0.58 ± 0.06 vs pQCD 0.61 in a restricted angular interval (not the symmetric "Mercedes" ratio; LEP-1 agreement with the antenna predictions is good but partly qualitative) ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)) | Rules out kinematic/independent-fragmentation origin; cannot separate drag from string (identical at large N_c) |
| Initial–final coherence, hadron colliders | CDF 1994 and D0 1997: HERWIG and angular-ordered PYTHIA favoured, ISAJET/no-AO excluded; tree-level O(αs³) nearly as good ([CDF_1994zkl](summaries/CDF_1994zkl.md), [D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md)). CMS 7 TeV (unfolded): same pattern, but **no generator fits**: χ²/17 dof central/forward PYTHIA6 2.5/8.1, PYTHIA8 1.7/6.4, HERWIG++ 1.2/3.5, MG+PY6 1.6/3.3; coherence off 7.7/11.5 ([CMS_2013cvx](summaries/CMS_2013cvx.md)). CMS 2021 3-jet/Z+2j: no generator describes both soft and hard emission ([CMS_2021hnp](summaries/CMS_2021hnp.md)) | Coherence-off strongly disfavoured; forward region unexplained by any generator |
| Jet vs boson (RQ1e) | D0 W+jet: R_sig ≈ 1.38 ± 0.10 (stat.), matched by AO+string and MLLA+LPHD, not AO-off ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)). PHENIX γ/π⁰–h: no colour-entanglement signal ([Osborn_2018bwn](summaries/Osborn_2018bwn.md)) | 390 events; entanglement search inconclusive |
| CR in e⁺e⁻ → WW (LEP2) | **LEP combination:** k_I = 1.26 (+0.84 −0.64) in SK-I, ~51 % reconnection at 189 GeV; no-CR disfavoured at 2.8σ (no single experiment > 2σ); 100 % reconnection excluded at 6.9σ. CR still a 35 MeV systematic on m_W(qqqq), so that channel gets 22 % weight. L3/DELPHI tension mostly from different no-CR reference MC ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)) | Only SK-I constrained directly; other models via "same fraction" argument |
| CR in hadron collisions | Indirect: models are tuned to ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch), dN/dη, Λ/K⁰_S ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md), [Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md)); jet pull in tt̄ insensitive to CR tune variation (≈0.2 %/bin) ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)) | No direct, model-independent CR measurement at hadron colliders yet |

**Emerging pattern.** (1) Coherence is established qualitatively in every collider environment, and coherence-free models fail everywhere. (2) But at hadron colliders the best generators still miss the precise unfolded data (CMS forward region), so "coherence is understood" is overstated. (3) CR is *favoured* at LEP2 (2.8σ, model-dependent) and *needed* to tune hadron-collider minimum bias, but no observable yet isolates it model-independently; its main practical impact is as a systematic on m_W and m_top.

## RQ4 — Experimental tests proposed in the theory literature

- Soft-hadron azimuthal asymmetries in γ+jet, W+jet, gq→gq events ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)); W+jet done by D0 ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)).
- Energy dependence of the hump-backed plateau peak in a restricted cone; kaons and baryons first ([Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), [Webber_1983if](summaries/Webber_1983if.md)).
- Particle flow in qg→qg vs W→qq̄ at hadron colliders ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md)).
- **Separating string and drag requires 1/N_c² observables** (azimuthal asymmetries, tagged-quark-jet tests) ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
- CR in tt̄: multiplicity and ΣpT outside b/W jets, ⟨pT⟩(n_ch), UE density, cone multiplicity around the top ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)).
- CR signatures in heavy-flavour baryons (charm/bottom baryon enhancement) and at future e⁺e⁻ colliders ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)); rapidity gaps at the Z as a test of string-reinteraction CR ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)).
- Jet pull with hadronic W in tt̄ as a colour-singlet calibration sample ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md)); done by ATLAS ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)).
- Queued: jet colour ring, H→bb̄ and VBF colour-flow radiation patterns, ZZ→bb̄cc̄ CR test, in-jet Λ_c/D⁰, rope/shoving signatures in small systems.
