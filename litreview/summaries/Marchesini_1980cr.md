# Space-time Description of Colour Screening via Jet Calculus Techniques
- **ID / links:** Marchesini:1980cr · INSPIRE 155295 · arXiv — · DOI 10.1016/0550-3213(81)90357-6
- **Authors / collab, year, journal:** G. Marchesini, L. Trentadue, G. Veneziano, 1981, Nucl. Phys. B 181 (1981) 335–346
- **Type:** theory
- **Relevance:** 2 · RQs: 1a, 2
- **Full text source:** zotero
## Takeaways
- Space-time counterpart of preconfinement: although generic partons are emitted at times growing ∝ Q (⟨t⟩ ~ xQ/Q_0² in the e⁺e⁻ cms), partons within one colour-singlet cluster are separated by a *finite* time O(1/Q_0) in the cluster rest frame (abstract; Sect. 3, p. 344–345).
- The lifetime of unscreened colour charge (time to screen the colour of the primary q̄) is finite, ⟨t^c.c.⟩ ~ E⟨x⟩_c.c./Q_0² ~ E/Q², i.e. O(1/Q_0) in the q̄ rest frame (Eq. 3.5, p. 344).
- Same dynamical origin as the finite cluster mass: colour-connected partons carry vanishing momentum fraction, ⟨x⟩_c.c. ≃ Q_0²/Q² (p. 336, 344).
- Wee partons are emitted earlier than fast ones (⟨t(x)⟩ → 0 for x → 0; Eqs. 2.16, 2.20), consistent with an inside–outside cascade (p. 342).
- Conclusion: the final state is a set of colour-singlet clusters local in both momentum and space-time, supporting hadronization acting locally — a key premise of cluster/string models and of the question of when and where colour reconnection may operate (p. 345).
## Setup
Theory; e⁺e⁻ at cms energy √Q² = 2E, LLA jet calculus in light-like axial gauge (gauge-independence shown in the Appendix for collinear gauges, Eq. A.1). Partons are "dressed" with virtuality k² > Q_0²; soft cutoff ε = max(Q_0²/k², k²/Q²ξ²) (Eq. 2.3). Key assumption: dressed-parton lifetime τ_i ≃ 1/√k_i², boosted by E_i/√k_i², so the time weight of a tree is t = Σ E_i/k_i² (Eqs. 2.4–2.5). An "off-shell final parton" of mass M is interpreted as depositing energy in a calorimeter cone of opening δ ≃ M/Q (p. 338, Fig. 3). Average times computed as ratios T/D via AP-type equations (Eqs. 2.6–2.9) and, for colour screening, using colour-connected (c.c.) distributions Γ (Eqs. 3.1–3.3, Fig. 5).
## Results
- Non-singlet (valence) quark, M² ~ Q_0²: ⟨t^NS⟩ ≈ (E/Q_0²)(c_F/2πb) I(Q_0²)[∫dz(1+x²/z²)D^NS(z)]/D^NS(x) (Eq. 2.13), with I(Q_0²) ~ 1/t_0 (Eq. 2.14).
- Limits: ⟨t^NS⟩ ~ (E/Q_0²)(c_F/2πb) x^α/(1−α) I as x → 0 (Eq. 2.16) and ~ (E/Q_0²)(c_F/2πb) 2(1−x)/(1+β) I as x → 1 (Eq. 2.17); the time rises with x, peaks and falls as x → 1 (no phase space for hard bremsstrahlung) (p. 342).
- Generic parton at large x: ⟨t⟩ ~ E/Q_0² (1−x) (Eq. 2.18); gluon at small x: ⟨t_q^g(x)⟩ ~ γ(E/M²) exp(−δ√log(1/x)) → 0 (Eqs. 2.19–2.20).
- C.c. distribution Γ_q^b ≈ Δ_q(Q²,Q_0²)δ(x−1) − (1/x)Q²∂Δ_q(xQ²,Q_0²)/∂Q² (Eq. 3.1); moments Eq. 3.2.
- Screening time of the primary colour: ⟨t^c.c.⟩ ~ (E/Q_0²)⟨x⟩_c.c. ≃ E/Q² (Eq. 3.5); in the frame where the non-radiating q̄ is at rest (E ≃ Q²/2Q_0) the screening partons are emitted after O(1/Q_0) (p. 344).
- General colour singlet from parent a of mass √k² (Fig. 6): T^qq̄(k²,Q_0²) ~ E/k² (Eq. 3.6, p. 345), i.e. T ~ 1/k in the parent rest frame; convoluted with the preconfinement k²-damping gives t^qq̄ ~ 1/Q_0 (p. 345).
- The first stage of evolution from Q² to Q Q_0 takes a time O(1/Q_0) that is not gauge-independently defined (Appendix, p. 346).
## Assessment
Results are order-of-magnitude, asymptotic estimates (up to ln ln k² factors, p. 345) resting on a heuristic lifetime assumption (Eq. 2.4) and on strongly ordered LLA without angular ordering; the gauge dependence of the earliest stage is explicitly unresolved. Still the earliest quantitative argument that colour-singlet systems are compact in space-time as well as momentum, which underlies (i) cluster/string hadronization as a local process, and (ii) later space-time arguments on whether colour reconnection between overlapping singlet systems (e.g. W⁺W⁻ at LEP2, Sjöstrand–Khoze; MPI in pp) can occur — the finite 1/Q_0 screening time limits the window for inter-system colour exchange. The application to nuclear DIS (formation time of secondary partons inside a nucleus, p. 337) links to coherence/formation-time ideas in jet quenching. No data comparison.
