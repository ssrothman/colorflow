# The String Effect and QCD Coherence
- **ID / links:** Azimov:1985zta · INSPIRE 228806 · arXiv — · DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(85)90709-9
- **Authors / collab, year, journal:** Ya.I. Azimov, Yu.L. Dokshitzer, V.A. Khoze, S.I. Troyan (LNPI Leningrad), 1985, Phys. Lett. B 165 (1985) 147–150 (received 20 May 1985, revised 12 Aug 1985)
- **Type:** theory (perturbative QCD + LPHD, with a data comparison)
- **Relevance:** 3 · RQs: 1b, 2, 3, 4
- **Full text source:** zotero (4 pp. scanned journal pages with text layer; read from page images)

## Takeaways
- This paper gives the **perturbative (coherence) explanation of the string effect**. Soft-gluon radiation at large angles in qq̄g is emitted coherently by the three-parton colour antenna (Eq. 4): W₊₋,₁ = N_c(H²₊₁ + H²₋₁) + (2C_F − N_c)H²₊₋. The q–q̄ term has a *negative* coefficient −1/N_c. Destructive interference cuts radiation between q and q̄ "by nearly a half" relative to qq̄γ (Fig. 2, p. 148).
- **Equivalence with the string picture (p. 148).** If the O(1/N_c²) term is dropped, the pattern is the incoherent sum of two qq̄-like dipoles (q–g and q̄–g), each boosted from its own rest frame. This "3 = 2 + 2 + Lorentz boosts" reproduces literally the Lund string explanation. The authors conclude that the "string and PT pictures appear to be rather close here" and that the Lund success reflects the fact that the string "catches quite well" the coherent PT physics (p. 147).
- **Prediction (p. 149).** Through MLLA + LPHD the ratio of non-jet flows is an infrared-safe, parameter-free asymptotic prediction, asymptotically independent of the total energy and of the colour interaction strength (Eq. 6, p. 149). For the typical JADE/TPC geometry (θ₁₂ = 155°, θ₁₃ = 130°, θ₂₃ = 75°):
  - r = N₁₃/N₁₂ = 2.56 if jet 3 is always the gluon;
  - r = 1.17 for an *incoherent* model that keeps the gluon's larger colour charge but drops interference (Eq. 7);
  - r = 1.62 for the realistic gluon-assignment mixture (p. 149).
  - So the effect comes from coherence, not from gluon dominance.
- **Comparison with data (Tabs. 1–2).** With the three-jet selection taken into account, r_PT = 1.50 (JADE) and 1.55 (TPC). Data give r_tot = 1.39 ± 0.04 (JADE) and 1.19 ± 0.05 (TPC). Out-of-plane-momentum and heavy-hadron selections give larger ratios: r_out = 1.73 ± 0.13 (JADE, p_out > 0.3 GeV); r_M = 1.9 ± 0.2 (JADE kaons) and 1.58 ± 0.28 (TPC K, p, Λ). The authors claim "reasonable quantitative agreement", with r_tot < r_PT < r_out, r_M expected and the gap closing as ~1/ln W (pp. 149–150).
- **Proposed tests (p. 150).**
  - Compare qq̄g with qq̄γ, to see the negative "third antenna".
  - Measure the string effect with the gluon jet identified, e.g. by heavy-quark tagging in QQ̄g. They also argue that the angular-ordered Webber–Marchesini MC "oversimplifies" inter-jet coherence and "should be modified" (pp. 147, 150).

## Setup
- **Process.** e⁺e⁻ → qq̄g with all inter-jet angles θ_ij ~ 1 and comparable jet energies. The quantity studied is the angular distribution of soft (K₂ ≪ E_i) non-jet hadrons at large angle to all jets (p. 147, Fig. 1).
- **Approximation.** Lowest-order soft-gluon antenna. Each jet is replaced by its parent parton, because large-angle soft radiation sees only the conserved total colour current (coherence, p. 147). Higher orders are handled by MLLA: 8π dN/dn₂ = (1/N_c) W₊₋,₁(n₂) (d/dY) N_g(Y), with Y ≈ ln(Eθ) and θ = min(θ₂₊, θ₂₋, θ₂₁). Relative corrections are O(√α_s(W)) (Eq. 6, p. 149).
- **Hadronization.** Local parton–hadron duality (LPHD): parton flows are compared directly to hadron flows (p. 147).
- **Radiation patterns.**
  - Reference qq̄γ pattern (Eq. 1): W₊₋ = 2C_F a₊₋/(a₂₊a₂₋), with a_ik = 1 − n_i·n_k. Its azimuthal projection onto the event plane after integrating θ₂ is V(α, β) (Eq. 2).
  - qq̄g projected pattern (Eq. 5): N_c[a₊₁V(α, γ) + a₁₋V(α, γ)] + (2C_F − N_c)a₊₋V(α, β). The second term in the bracket is printed as a₁₋V(α, γ) in the scan. By symmetry we would expect the angles measured from the q̄ and the gluon (e.g. V(β, θ₁₋ + β)) in this term, so this may be a misprint. We have not re-derived it, and the scan is unambiguous about what is printed.
  - The incoherent comparison model (Eq. 7): W = C_F(2/a₂₊ + 2/a₂₋) + N_c·2/a₂₁.
- **Observable.** r = N₁₃/N₁₂, with N_ik = ∫₀.₃^0.₇ (dN_ik/dx) dx and x = φ/θ_ik the normalised angle from jet i (p. 149). Jets are energy-ordered (1, 2 most energetic). The mixture of gluon assignments is Φ₃ : Φ₂ : Φ₁ = 70% : 24% : 5% (p. 149, from refs. [5,13]).
- **Data.** JADE (Z. Phys. C 21 (1983) 37; Petersen, DESY 84-082) and TPC (LBL-18408, 1984), both at PETRA/PEP energies (p. 149).

## Results
- **Fig. 2 (directivity diagrams in the event plane, r = ln 2W(φ)).** The qq̄g flow opposite the gluon is "appreciably lower" than in qq̄γ. Interference cancels radiation between q and q̄ "by nearly a half" (p. 148).
- **Pure-gluon case, typical geometry.** Coherent r = 2.56 vs incoherent r = 1.17 (p. 149). With the selection accounted for (Tab. 2), g = jet 3 gives 2.60 (JADE) and 2.59 (TPC). The incoherent model gives 1.16 for both.
- **All configurations (Tab. 2).** r_PT = 1.50 (JADE) and 1.55 (TPC); the incoherent model gives 1.05 for both. The text quotes 1.62 for the average geometry before selection effects (p. 149).
- **Data (Tab. 1).**

  | sample | JADE | TPC |
  |---|---|---|
  | all hadrons, r_tot | 1.39 ± 0.04 | 1.19 ± 0.05 |
  | all-p_out π± | — | 1.17 ± 0.06 |
  | p_out > 0.3 GeV, r_out | 1.73 ± 0.13 | — |
  | 0.3 < p_out < 0.5 GeV | — | 1.67 ± 0.24 |
  | K mesons, r_M | 1.9 ± 0.2 | — |
  | heavy (K, p, Λ) | — | 1.58 ± 0.28 |

- **Authors' interpretation of the r_tot deficit (p. 149).** It is attributed to the "non-dual" component, e.g. decays of slow resonances, which is roughly isotropic and dilutes the effect. They expect r_PT − r_tot = O(1/N(W)).
- **Authors' interpretation of the rise with M⊥ = √(M² + p²_out).** It reflects collectivity, i.e. the Lorentz boost of incoherent subsystems (strings *or* PT antennas), regardless of the form of the k⊥ distribution. A lowest-order estimate gives r_out(r_M) − r_PT ~ 1/ln W (pp. 149–150).

## Assessment
- **The pivot paper.** It turns the string effect from evidence *for the Lund string* into a consequence of **perturbative colour coherence**: the interference term with the −1/N_c coefficient. The comparison of r = 2.56 (coherent) with 1.17 (incoherent with gluon dominance) is the cleanest demonstration that interference, not the larger gluon colour charge, drives the effect.
- **Agreement with data is looser than claimed.**
  - r_PT = 1.50 vs JADE r_tot = 1.39 ± 0.04 is about a 2.8σ difference (statistical error only, our arithmetic).
  - TPC r_tot = 1.19 ± 0.05 is ~7σ below r_PT = 1.55.
  - The non-dual correction that is supposed to account for this is argued for, not computed. The bracketing r_tot < r_PT < r_out, r_M is a qualitative consistency, not a prediction.
  - The JADE and TPC r_tot values disagree with each other by ~3σ (our arithmetic). Selections differ, so this need not be a real tension.
- **Discrimination string vs PT.** The authors themselves show that at leading N_c the two pictures share the same structure: two boosted dipoles, matching Andersson:1980vk's two string pieces. At fixed PETRA energy the r value therefore cannot separate them; even the M⊥ dependence is attributed to both. Two things differ:
  - PT predicts an energy- and Λ-independent asymptotic r for all hadrons (Eq. 6). In the string picture the p_out/M dependence comes from a fixed-scale boost.
  - PT includes the O(1/N_c²) negative q–q̄ term. The string, and leading-colour dipole showers, do not have it.

  Khoze:1989km later quantifies the first point with MC.
- **Comparison with Andersson:1980vk.** The Lund paper predicts the same physical asymmetry, but as an event-by-event gluon tag at 30 GeV. It gives no r value and no coherence argument. Azimov et al. supply the first parameter-free number and the gauge-theory origin.
- **Comparison with Khoze:1989km.** That paper is co-authored by Khoze and uses ARIADNE (leading-colour dipoles, i.e. this paper's "2 + 2" approximation) plus JETSET strings. Its MLLA+LPHD values (Table 1 there) for the three-fold symmetric geometry are r ≈ 2.64–2.75, a different geometry from the 2.56 here.
- **Later confirmation.** The heavy-quark-tagged measurement proposed here was carried out by OPAL (OPAL_1991xwi): R̃ = 1.62 ± 0.07 with lepton-tagged gluon jets at LEP1. Like this paper, OPAL did not separate the PT and string explanations.
- **Caveats.** Asymptotic, soft-gluon, large-angle approximation with energy-ordered jets. The scan's Eq. 5 contains a possible misprint, noted under Setup; it does not affect the numerical results as quoted. Ref. [13] (Yad. Fiz. 42 (1985)) holds the detailed calculation of Tabs. 1–2 and was not read here.
