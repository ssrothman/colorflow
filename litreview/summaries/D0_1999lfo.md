# Evidence of color coherence effects in W+jets events from ppbar collisions at sqrt(s) = 1.8 TeV
- **ID / links:** D0:1999lfo · INSPIRE 505114 · arXiv hep-ex/9908017 · DOI 10.1016/S0370-2693(99)01015-1
- **Authors / collab, year, journal:** D0 Collaboration (B. Abbott et al.), 1999, Phys. Lett. B
- **Type:** experimental
- **Relevance:** 3 · RQs: 1b, 1e, 2, 3
- **Full text source:** zotero

## Takeaways
- First hadron-collider measurement of coherence between initial and final state that uses a colorless boson as an in-event reference. Soft calorimeter-tower activity in an annulus around the leading jet is compared with the same annulus around the W (p. 5–6, Fig. 1).
- The jet/W tower-multiplicity ratio is > 1 at all azimuthal angles β around the axis. It is largest in the event plane (β = 0, π: ≈ 1.6 and ≈ 1.45) and smallest in the transverse plane (β = π/2: ≈ 1.1) (≈ read from Fig. 3b). This is the "string/drag"-type interjet pattern expected for initial-to-final color interference.
- The signal variable R_sig = (event-plane ratio)/(transverse-plane ratio) is ≈ 1.38 ± 0.10 (stat.) for data (≈ read from Fig. 5; the paper quotes no central value, only the 7 % stat. error, p. 9). Model values, ≈ (read from Fig. 5):
  - PYTHIA 5.7 with angular ordering (AO) and string fragmentation (SF): ≈ 1.30.
  - AO off + SF: ≈ 1.11.
  - AO off + independent fragmentation (IF): ≈ 1.02.
  - MLLA+LPHD (Khoze–Stirling): ≈ 1.44.
  So string fragmentation alone does not reproduce the effect; perturbative AO is needed (p. 9).
- An analytic MLLA+LPHD parton-level calculation (Khoze & Stirling, Z. Phys. C76 (1997) 59) describes the shape of the β pattern without hadronization or underlying-event modelling (Fig. 4d). The authors take this as support for LPHD.
- The statistics are small (390 events, 85 pb⁻¹; p. 6, 5), so this is "evidence", not a precision constraint. It is a key template for jet–boson color-flow observables (RQ1e).

## Setup
- Tevatron Run I (1994–95), p p̄ at √s = 1.8 TeV, 85 pb⁻¹, W → eν (p. 5).
- **Selections** (p. 5–6):
  - W: E_T^e > 25 GeV, missing E_T > 25 GeV, |η_e| < 1.1, 40 < M_T < 110 GeV, isolation f_iso < 0.1. Of the two neutrino p_z solutions, the one giving the smaller |y_W| is used (correct about 2/3 of the time per MC).
  - Jets: cone R = 0.7. The tagged jet is the leading-E_T jet in the azimuthal hemisphere opposite the W, with E_T > 10 GeV. Also required: p_T^W > 10 GeV, |η_jet| < 0.7, |y_W| < 0.7.
  - Single reconstructed vertex within 20 cm, and beam–beam hodoscope timing consistent with one interaction, to suppress multiple interactions. Main Ring pileup events are removed.
  - 390 events remain.
- **Observable:** count of calorimeter towers (0.1×0.1 in η×φ) with E_T > 250 MeV in an annulus 0.7 < R < 1.5 around the W and around the jet. Each tower is binned in polar angle β = tan⁻¹(sign(η_X)·Δφ/Δη), with β = 0 pointing along the beam direction nearest to the object (p. 6, Fig. 1).
  - Where the two annuli overlap, a tower goes to the nearer object.
  - Cryostat-gap towers (1.1 < |η| < 1.4) and electron-cluster towers (R < 0.3) are excluded.
  - Tower E_T values are offset-corrected for noise, zero suppression and pileup (p. 7).
- **R_sig** = [N_jet(bin1)+N_jet(bin7)]/[N_W(bin1)+N_W(bin7)] ÷ [N_jet(bin4)/N_W(bin4)] (p. 8). It is expected to be about 1 without coherence, and it is insensitive to overall normalization.
- **Models:**
  - PYTHIA 5.7, run through full GEANT D0 simulation with data noise overlaid, in three configurations: (i) AO on + SF (perturbative plus non-perturbative coherence); (ii) AO off + SF (non-perturbative only); (iii) AO off + IF (no coherence) (p. 6–7).
  - The analytic MLLA+LPHD prediction (Khoze–Stirling) assumes central W and parton production and includes no underlying event or detector simulation (p. 8).
  - All predictions are normalized to the integral of the data β distribution (p. 8; Fig. 4 caption says "normalized to the data").

## Results
- **Control distributions:** PYTHIA (AO+SF) agrees well with data in electron E_T, missing E_T, Δφ(W, jet) and |y_W| − |η_jet| (Fig. 2, p. 7).
- **Raw β distributions:** both the jet and the W distributions peak around π/2 < β < 3π/4. The authors attribute this to both process dynamics and phase-space effects from the event selection and the tower E_T threshold (Fig. 3a, p. 7). Jet-side multiplicity exceeds W-side multiplicity in every bin.
- **Jet/W ratio:** 7 bins of width π/7. ≈ 1.6 in the first bin (centre π/14), falling to ≈ 1.1 at β ≈ π/2, then rising to ≈ 1.45 in the last bin (centre 13π/14). Errors are statistical only, ≈ ±0.05–0.13 per bin (≈ read from Fig. 3b).
- **Model comparisons (Fig. 4):**
  - AO on + SF follows the data in all bins (4a).
  - AO off + SF is flatter. It undershoots the β ≈ 0 and π bins and overshoots in the bins from ≈ π/4 to π/2 (4b).
  - AO off + IF is essentially flat at ≈ 1.3 (≈ read from Fig. 4c) and does not reproduce the modulation (4c).
  - The MLLA+LPHD curve reproduces the shape (4d).
- **R_sig (Fig. 5):** values as in the Takeaways (data ≈ 1.38, statistical band ≈ 1.28–1.48; ≈ read from Fig. 5). Only PYTHIA AO+SF (≈ 1.31, near the lower edge) and MLLA+LPHD (≈ 1.44) lie inside the data band. AO off + SF is ≈ 2.8σ low and AO off + IF ≈ 3.7σ low (reviewer's estimate from the data stat. error only; the paper quotes no significance, it says only "in disagreement", p. 9).
- **Systematics (p. 9):**
  - Dijet background is about 5 %, giving a 1–2 % uncertainty on R_sig.
  - Offset correction contributes about 1 %.
  - No dependence of R_sig on luminosity was observed.
  - The statistical error (7 %) dominates.
- **Tower threshold dependence (Fig. 6, p. 9–10):** the threshold was varied over 200–350 MeV.
  - Values below are ≈ (read from Fig. 6), at 200/250/300/350 MeV.
  - Data R_sig ≈ 1.34, 1.38, 1.29 and 1.20.
  - AO+SF ≈ 1.31 → 1.26.
  - AO off + SF ≈ 1.14 → 1.03.
  - AO off + IF ≈ 1.06 → 0.98.
  - AO+SF is the closest model at every threshold (p. 10). Both data and MC tend downward as the threshold rises (data not monotonic: 250 MeV is highest); the paper only says there "seems to be a dependence".

## Assessment
- **Clean idea:** using the colorless W as an in-event reference cancels much of the underlying event and detector response. It is a more direct extraction than the earlier 3-jet η/β studies (CDF 1994; D0 1997), which relied entirely on MC-on/off comparisons.
- **Statistics:** the sample is very small (390 events) and only statistical errors are shown in the figures. The separation from the no-AO models is ≈ 2.8–3.7σ (reviewer's estimate, stat. only), so this is evidence rather than an observation (though the paper's conclusion says the effects "have been observed", p. 10).
- **Asymmetric reference:** the "W side" is not a pure reference. The W annulus is defined around a reconstructed W direction, with a twofold y_W ambiguity resolved correctly only about 2/3 of the time, and it contains the incoming-parton radiation pattern. The jet/W ratio therefore probes the full initial-plus-final dipole pattern, not only the jet. MC carries the ambiguity consistently, but the analytic MLLA calculation assumes exact central kinematics.
- **Caveats on the MLLA+LPHD comparison:**
  - It omits the underlying event, detector effects and the 250 MeV threshold. Its agreement is in the normalized shape and in R_sig only, so it may be partly fortuitous (reviewer's opinion; the paper calls it consistent with the data and supportive of LPHD).
  - The MC models were normalized to data, so only shapes are tested.
- **Era of the models:** this is PYTHIA 5.7 with Lund strings, before modern dipole/CR models. "AO off" also changes the overall shower, not only the interference, so the on/off comparison mixes coherence with general shower changes.
- **Absent effects:** color reconnection and MPI color effects were not considered. Modern generators with MPI+CR might alter the soft pattern.
- **Interpretation:** the result is consistent with the string/drag picture of color flow between the incoming partons and the outgoing jet. It is a natural Tevatron analogue of e⁺e⁻ string-effect measurements and a precursor to LHC jet-pull and Z/γ+jet soft-radiation studies (RQ1e/RQ4).
- **Status:** it is not known to be in HEPData or Rivet (not checked).
