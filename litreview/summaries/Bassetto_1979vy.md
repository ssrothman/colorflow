# Color Singlet Distributions and Mass Damping in Perturbative QCD
- **ID / links:** Bassetto:1979vy · INSPIRE 140217 · arXiv — · DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(79)90687-7
- **Authors / collab, year, journal:** A. Bassetto, M. Ciafaloni, G. Marchesini, 1979, Phys. Lett. B 83 (1979) 207–212
- **Type:** theory
- **Relevance:** 2 · RQs: 1a, 2
- **Full text source:** zotero
## Takeaways
- Generalises Amati–Veneziano preconfinement from the "first" (leading-quark) colour singlet to *all* colour-singlet clusters in a quark jet, via a generating/partition functional in jet calculus (Eqs. 1–3, Figs. 1–2).
- Introduces "colour line connecting" densities Γ_a^q: distributions where the quark colour line ends on the initial parton through gluon emission only (p. 210, Figs. 4, 6) — an early formal colour-flow (colour-connection) object.
- Every colour-singlet cluster has a power-damped mass spectrum, at least (M²/Q_0²)^(-C_F/πb), and away from the wee region (M²/Q_0²)^(-2C_F/πb) (Eqs. 19–20, p. 211–212).
- Number of colour-singlet clusters is tied to parton multiplicity: N_a^S ≈ ½ n_a^q (Eq. 12), with N_q^S/N_g^S ≈ C_F/C_A (p. 211) — proposed as experimentally testable if clusters ↔ hadrons.
- Mechanism: soft IR divergences do not cancel between real and virtual terms once the colour-singlet (no-qq̄-pair) selection is imposed; analogous to Sudakov/Cornwall–Tiktopoulos form-factor damping (p. 207, 212).
## Setup
Theory; LLA jet calculus for a single-parton jet (e.g. photon-triggered), P → ∞ frame, final partons at virtuality Q_0², evolution variable τ = ln Q²/Λ² (p. 207–208). Partition functional G_a with parton fugacities z_b (Eq. 2) obeys Eq. 3 with virtual potential V_a and real-emission vertex P̂; soft limits fixed by kinematics z > k_1²/k², 1−z > k_2²/k² (Eq. 4). Zero moment and nonzero x-moments of the colour-connecting densities treated separately.
## Results
- Virtual potentials: V_q = (C_F/πb)[−(τ−τ_0) + 3/4], V_g = −(C_A/πb)(τ−τ_0) + 1 (Eq. 5); linear growth in τ−τ_0 is the IR-singular piece that fails to cancel in semi-inclusive quantities.
- Probability σ_g that a gluon emits only gluons: σ_g ≈ exp[−(C_A/πb)(τ−τ_0)](τ/τ_0)^(1+(C_A/πb)B) (Eq. 8), driven to zero by the −δσ_g² term, δ = N_f/6πb (Eq. 7).
- Quark emitting only gluons: φ^q ≈ exp[−(C_F/πb)(τ−τ_0)](τ/τ_0)^((C_F/πb)(B+3/4)) (Eq. 9), reproducing AV; first-singlet parent-mass spectrum ~(k²/Q_0²)^(-C_F/πb) (Eq. 11).
- Sum rules Γ_q^q(n=0)=1, Γ_g^q(n=0)=1−σ_g (Eq. 10); for n ≠ 0, Γ^q(n) ~ exp[−(C_F/πb)(τ−τ_0)] (Eq. 16); the x → 0 behaviour Γ_g^q ~ x^(-(1+C_F/πb)) e^(−(C_F/πb)(τ−τ_0)) (Eq. 17) compensates the damping in the zero moment — colour-connected partons are concentrated at small x.
- Singlet multiplicity: N_a^S ≈ ½ n_a^q (Eq. 12); asymptotic n ~ exp[2(C_A/πb)^(1/2)(√τ − √τ_0)] and n_q/n_g ≈ C_F/C_A (Eq. 13).
- qq̄ singlet mass spectrum M² dσ/dM²dx_1dx_2 ≈ f(x_1,x_2)(M²/Q_0²)^(-2C_F/πb) (Eq. 19), twice the AV exponent; kinematic bound ⟨M²⟩ < ⟨k²(x_1+x_2)⟩ guarantees at least exponent C_F/πb for any colourless cluster (Eq. 20, p. 212).
- Authors note the precise exponent (2C_F/πb vs C_F/πb) is "not crucial" and will be enhanced by non-perturbative effects near Q_0 (p. 212).
## Assessment
Short letter; full derivations deferred to Nucl. Phys. B163 (1980) 477 (ref. [5]). Results are asymptotic, leading-log, strongly virtuality ordered and essentially leading-N_c (colour lines followed through gluons, qq̄ splitting as singlet break); no angular ordering/coherence. The wee-x singularity (Eq. 17) may "eat up" part of the damping (p. 211–212), so the stronger exponent is not guaranteed. The colour-connecting density Γ is conceptually the precursor of colour-flow tracking in showers and of cluster formation in HERWIG; the singlet-multiplicity ∝ parton-multiplicity relation (Eq. 12) is the perturbative underpinning of local parton–hadron duality ideas. No data comparison. Relevant to RQ2 as theory foundation of cluster models and the planar colour-connection picture that CR models later perturb.
