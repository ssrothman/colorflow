# Preconfinement as a Property of Perturbative QCD
- **ID / links:** Amati:1979fg · INSPIRE 140101 · arXiv — · DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(79)90896-7
- **Authors / collab, year, journal:** D. Amati, G. Veneziano (CERN), 1979, Phys. Lett. B 83 (1979) 87–92
- **Type:** theory
- **Relevance:** 2 · RQs: 1a, 2
- **Full text source:** zotero
## Takeaways
- Origin of the "preconfinement" concept: at leading-log (LLA) in axial gauge the perturbative cascade is planar, so final partons are colour-ordered and can be grouped into adjacent colour-singlet clusters (q, q̄ + gluons), exact singlets at large N_c (p. 87–88, Fig. 1).
- The mass of such colour-singlet clusters is power-damped, ~(M_C/Q_0)^(-λ), so the average cluster mass is finite and O(Q_0), independent of the hard scale Q (p. 88; Eqs. 18, 20, 23).
- The damping comes from an *uncompensated soft (IR) divergence*: requiring "no qq̄ pair emission" removes real contributions but not virtual ones; a φ³₆ toy model without soft divergences gives only logarithmic damping, i.e. ⟨M⟩ ~ Q (Eqs. 8–9, p. 90).
- Physical picture: hadronization as local "fusion" of colour-singlet clusters at scales < Q_0 rather than independent fragmentation of single partons — the conceptual root of the cluster hadronization model and of the colour-connection (leading-colour) picture behind string/cluster models and later colour reconnection (p. 92).
- Ordering by colour adjacency (planarity) is the same leading-N_c structure that defines colour flow / colour dipoles in parton showers.
## Setup
Theory only; e⁺e⁻ → hadrons quark jets in LLA ("jet calculus" of Konishi–Ukawa–Veneziano), light-like/axial gauge, large-N_c limit with α_s N_c and N_f/N_c fixed (footnote 2, p. 88). Defines modified propagators D̃_g, D̃_q for gluon/quark evolution with emission of gluons only (no qq̄ pairs); their non-linear self-consistency equations (Fig. 3) generalise Altarelli–Parisi. Spectrum of the first cluster C_1 obtained from Fig. 2a with two identifications (I1, I2) of the mixed propagator D̃_qg (Fig. 2b). Soft cut-off ε ≈ p'²/p² from kinematics (p. 90–91, Eq. 11). Toy comparison: SU(N) φ³ in six dimensions (Eq. 1).
## Results
- φ³₆ toy model: D̃_g → (1/δ)(log p²/m²)^(-1/9) (Eq. 6), δ = N_f/N; first-cluster mass spectrum has only an extra logarithmic damping (Eq. 8), average cluster mass remains O(Q) (p. 90).
- In QCD the virtual coefficients diverge, A_R^q ≈ 2N_c log(p'²/p²) (Eqs. 10–11), turning log damping into power damping: D̃_g ~ (p²/Q_0²)^(-N_c/πb)(log p²)^a (Eq. 18), with b = (11N_c − 2N_f)/12π (Eq. 14).
- D̃_q ≈ [(log p²)^δ D̃_g]^(C_F/C_A) (Eq. 19); first-cluster mass spectrum ∝ α(p²)/p² · (p²/Q_0²)^(-C_F/πb) for I1 and ^(-(C_F+C_A)/πb) for I2 (Eq. 20); normalisation check Eq. 21 holds without explicit solutions.
- Damping exponent N_c/πb = 12/(11 − 2N_f/N_c) ≳ 1 (4/3 for N_f = N_c = 3) (Eq. 23), giving a cluster mass distribution ~(M_C/Q_0)^(-4), hence finite ⟨M_C⟩ ~ Q_0 (p. 92).
- Average cluster mass decreases with N_f/N_c, since δ acts as the driving term (p. 92).
- Prediction: D̃_q²(Q²) ∝ (Q²/Q_0²)^(-(N_c²−1)/πbN_c) for σ(e⁺e⁻ → qq̄ + gluons only)/σ(all) (p. 92).
- Note added: Bassetto–Ciafaloni–Marchesini confirm the conjecture that D̃_qg gives extra damping ~ D̃_g^(1/2) (p. 92).
## Assessment
Foundational, not quantitative: the solution of Eq. 16 is only asymptotic, constants a, α are undetermined (p. 91), the true damping is only bracketed between I1 and I2 (p. 92), and the argument is strictly leading-log, leading-N_c and strongly ordered in virtuality (no angular ordering — coherence was introduced only later by Mueller/Ermolaev–Fadin/Marchesini–Webber). Finite-N_c colour-singlet ambiguity is acknowledged: singlet grouping is not unique and "any fundamental role played by singlets" lies in non-perturbative physics (p. 88) — exactly the ambiguity that colour reconnection models later exploit. For this review it is the theoretical origin of the leading-colour, planar colour-connection structure underlying cluster/string models (RQ2) and supports the view that colour-singlet systems are local in momentum space (RQ1). No experimental content.
