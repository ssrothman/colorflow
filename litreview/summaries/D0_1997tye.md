# Color coherent radiation in multijet events from p p̄ collisions at √s = 1.8 TeV
- **ID / links:** D0:1997tye · INSPIRE 444264 · arXiv hep-ex/9706012 · DOI 10.1016/S0370-2693(97)01190-8
- **Authors / collab, year, journal:** D0 Collaboration (B. Abbott et al.), 1997, Phys. Lett. B 414, 419–427
- **Type:** experimental
- **Relevance:** 3 · RQs: 1b, 1d, 2, 3
- **Full text source:** zotero (journal PDF; anchors are journal pages 419–427)

## Takeaways
- Confirms and extends CDF:1994zkl: in 3-jet events the third jet around the second jet is enhanced near the event plane (β = 0, π: plane of jet 2 and beam) and depleted in the transverse plane (β = π/2) relative to ISAJET (no coherence), as expected from initial-to-final state color interference (Fig. 4, p. 425).
- The data's enhancement near β = π is "more dramatic" in the forward region 0.7 < |η2| < 1.5 than centrally |η2| < 0.7 (Fig. 3, p. 424; this is the raw shape, which includes phase-space/selection effects — the Data/ISAJET χ² is actually smaller forward, 24.9 vs 48.0) — first measurement in the forward region (p. 423).
- Quantified with χ²/8 dof of Data/MC ratio fitted to a constant (Tab. 2): HERWIG 5.8 (5.1, 7.7) and JETRAD O(αs³) (7.9, 7.9) good; PYTHIA 5.7 with AO (12.7, 11.3) acceptable with residual near-beam differences; ISAJET (48.0, 24.9) "clearly disagrees" and PYTHIA without AO (77.2, 62.9) is significantly different [central, forward]. ("Good/acceptable" are this summary's reading; the paper only says HERWIG and JETRAD "agree best".)
- String fragmentation alone (PYTHIA AO off) cannot produce the effect; it must come from the perturbative stage (AO in the shower, or fixed-order 2→3 matrix element) — supports local parton-hadron duality (p. 426–427).
- First direct comparison of such coherence observables to an analytic NLO-type (tree-level 3-jet, O(αs³)) parton calculation, which describes the data nearly as well as HERWIG (χ² 7.9/7.9 vs 5.1/7.7; equal 7.2 combined); the authors call HERWIG the best representation (p. 427).

## Setup
- Tevatron Run Ia (1992–93), √s = 1.8 TeV, 8 pb⁻¹; three-level trigger; final online (processor-farm) stage required a jet with E_T > 85 GeV (p. 423).
- Jets: iterative fixed cone R = 0.5, E_T corrected for UE/multiple interactions/noise, out-of-cone showering, response (missing-E_T projection fraction); |z_vtx| < 50 cm (p. 423).
- Selection: ≥ 3 jets, E_T1 > 115 GeV, E_T3 > 15 GeV, π/2 < Δφ21 < 3π/2; third jet in annulus 0.6 < R < π/2 around jet 2 (R = √(Δη32²+Δφ32²)). Two regions |η2| < 0.7 (9 048 events) and 0.7 < |η2| < 1.5 (5 776 events); η1 unconstrained (p. 423).
- Observable: β = tan⁻¹(sign(η2)·Δφ32/Δη32), β = 0 toward the beam nearer jet 2, β = π toward the far beam, normalized 1/N dN/dβ (Fig. 2, p. 423).
- Models: ISAJET 7.13 (independent shower, no coherence), HERWIG 5.8 (AO, cluster hadronization), PYTHIA 5.7 with AO on/off (string fragmentation; I-F coherence implemented), JETRAD 1.2 O(αs³) parton level (p. 424). Showered MCs at hadron level, JETRAD at parton level; detector η and energy resolution effects included in all predictions (method not specified; "parametrized" is an inference), same selection as data (p. 424).

## Results
- β distributions rise from β = 0 to β = π in both regions; rise is steeper forward (1/N dN/dβ ≈ 0.06 → ≈ 0.18, ≈ (read from Fig. 3)) than centrally (≈ 0.10 → ≈ 0.14, ≈ (read from Fig. 3)). HERWIG agrees in both; ISAJET deviates systematically (Fig. 3).
- Data/ISAJET ratio > 1 near β = 0 and π, < 1 near β = π/2 in both regions; Data/PYTHIA(AO off) shows a larger excess near β = 0 (≈ 1.3–1.5) and a deficit (≈ 0.8) from β ≈ π/2 onward, with only ≈ 0.9–1.0 near β = π, ≈ (read from Fig. 4b,g); Data/HERWIG and Data/JETRAD flat near 1; Data/PYTHIA(AO on) much closer to 1 but with "residual differences in the near beam region" (Fig. 4, panels a–j; p. 425).
- χ² (8 dof) of Data/MC to constant, central / forward / combined |η2| < 1.5: ISAJET 48.0 / 24.9 / 74.4; HERWIG 5.1 / 7.7 / 7.2; JETRAD 7.9 / 7.9 / 7.2; PYTHIA no AO 77.2 / 62.9 / 126.0; PYTHIA with AO 12.7 / 11.3 / 19.3 (Tab. 2). Statistical plus uncorrelated systematic included, and MC jet-energy resolution uncertainty.
- Data uncertainties (central / forward): jet energy scale 2.2% / 3.4%; η bias 1.2% / 1.7%; reconstruction efficiency near beam +1.5% / +5.0%, far beam −0.5% / −2.0%; statistical 3.2% / 4.3% (Tab. 1).
- MC systematics: jet-resolution variation < 2.6% (2.8%) central (forward); CTEQ2ML/MF/MS PDFs < 2.0%; JETRAD scale E_T1/2–2E_T1 < 1.3% (2.5%) (p. 426).
- HERWIG parton vs hadron level: Data/HERWIG ratios same → hadronization effects negligible as modeled by HERWIG; limited GEANT full-simulation sample shows residual detector effects negligible (p. 426).
- Authors conclude: strong initial-to-final state interference; AO-based showers reproduce data, HERWIG best; striking disagreement for models without coherence; 2→3 parton calculation good, supporting LPHD (p. 426–427).

## Assessment
- More quantitative than CDF (χ², systematics table, two η regions, fixed-order comparison), but still a detector-level, shape-only comparison with only parametrized detector smearing for most MCs; results not unfolded, so not directly reusable for tuning (no HEPData expected for 1997).
- The JETRAD agreement is notable: a tree-level 2→3 matrix element contains the full color interference at that order, so the "coherence" signature here is largely the exact LO 3-jet radiation pattern — showing this observable tests wide-angle hard emission (the third jet has E_T > 15 GeV) rather than soft-gluon coherence in the strict sense. Coherence in MC = getting the first hard ISR emission's angular pattern right.
- Hadronization insensitivity is shown only within HERWIG (cluster); the PYTHIA AO-off comparison shows string fragmentation does not mimic the effect, which is a more model-independent statement.
- The PYTHIA (AO on) χ² ≈ 12 for 8 dof leaves mild tension; given ~5% systematics near the beam forward, not decisive.
- Consistency with CDF:1994zkl: both find HERWIG good, ISAJET and coherence-less PYTHIA bad. CDF tested PYTHIA 5.6 (no I-F coherence) failing and the private PYTHIA+ succeeding; D0 tested PYTHIA 5.7, i.e. the release containing that fix, and finds it acceptable — the two are a coherent story. D0's smaller cone (0.5 vs 0.7), higher E_T3 (15 vs 10 GeV) and E_T1 (115 vs 110 GeV), UE-corrected jets and forward region make it complementary.
