# Open questions and ideas lacking convincing experimental validation

_Status: after iteration 1 (2026-10-30). Each item cites the summaries that raise it._

## Interpretation

1. **Perturbative coherence or string?** The e⁺e⁻ string effect is established as dynamical (not kinematic), but the data cannot separate the perturbative drag effect (with LPHD) from Lund-string hadronization; both rest on the same leading-N_c colour flow ([OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md), [Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md)). Is there any observable that does?
2. **What do the Tevatron coherence observables actually test?** A tree-level O(αs³) calculation describes the D0 β distribution almost as well as HERWIG, so the effect may be mostly the wide-angle pattern of the first hard emission rather than soft-gluon coherence ([D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md), [CDF_1994zkl](summaries/CDF_1994zkl.md)). CDF also finds final-state angular ordering alone insufficient. Needs a check against the CMS 7 TeV measurement (queued).
3. **Size of subleading-colour (1/N_c²) effects.** Coherent showers drop non-planar terms, claimed "few percent" on two configurations only ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md), [Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md)). Beyond-leading-colour shower papers are queued to see whether any data show a measurable effect.

## Colour reconnection

4. **Model coverage of LEP2 limits.** Particle-flow results constrain SK-I but are insensitive to SK-II, ARIADNE and HERWIG CR ([L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md)). Is CR in WW actually "constrained", or only one model?
5. **L3 vs DELPHI tension.** L3 favours no CR (P_reco < 64 % at 95 % CL); DELPHI's combined fit gives P_reco ≈ 52 % (31–68 %) and disfavours no CR at only ≈1.9σ ([L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md), [DELPHI_2006tie](summaries/DELPHI_2006tie.md)). What does the LEP-wide combination conclude? (ALEPH:2013dgf and the LEP CR combination are queued.)
6. **CR uncertainty on m_top.** "CR on − CR off" in default Pythia 8 likely underestimates it; new models give ~±0.5 GeV after tuning ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md)). The observables proposed there to constrain CR in tt̄ need to be matched to actual measurements (ATLAS:2022ctr, CMS underlying-event-in-tt̄, queued).
7. **Jet pull and CR.** ATLAS jet pull separates colour-singlet from colour-octet W, but the CR tune variation moves it by only ~0.2 % per bin (hadronisation model: 0.55 %), and no generator describes all pull observables ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)). Why do generators overpredict colour-flow effects in pull?

## Statistical / methodological

8. Most classic coherence measurements are detector-level, shape-only, with statistical errors only (CDF, D0 3-jet, OPAL) ([CDF_1994zkl](summaries/CDF_1994zkl.md), [D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md), [OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md)). Are there unfolded, HEPData-archived versions usable for modern generator tests?
9. The D0 W+jet evidence rests on 390 events ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)). Has jet–boson colour coherence ever been measured at the LHC with high statistics (e.g. CMS:2021hnp, Z+2 jets, queued)?
