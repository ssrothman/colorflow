# Open questions and ideas lacking convincing experimental validation

_Status: after iteration 2 (2026-10-30). Each item cites the summaries that raise it._

## Interpretation

1. **Perturbative coherence or string?** The string effect is established as dynamical, but at large N_c the antenna/drag picture and the Lund string coincide, so leading-colour data cannot separate them. Only colour-suppressed (1/N_c²) effects can, and these were unobserved as of 1996 ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md), [OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md), [JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md)). Has any later measurement seen a 1/N_c² colour effect?
2. **Hadron-collider coherence is not fully described.** Tevatron data favoured HERWIG, but at 7 TeV no generator fits the unfolded β distribution, especially forward (best χ²/17 dof ≈ 3.3–3.5), and a tree-level calculation did nearly as well at the Tevatron ([CMS_2013cvx](summaries/CMS_2013cvx.md), [D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md), [CMS_2021hnp](summaries/CMS_2021hnp.md)). Is the forward discrepancy coherence, ISR modelling, or matching?
3. **Size of subleading-colour effects in showers.** Coherent showers drop 1/N² terms ("few percent", shown on two configurations) ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md), [Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md)). Beyond-leading-colour shower papers are queued.

## Colour reconnection

4. **LEP2 CR is favoured but only within one model.** LEP combination: k_I = 1.26 (+0.84 −0.64), no-CR disfavoured at 2.8σ, but only SK-I is constrained directly; other models enter via a "same reconnection fraction" argument, and individual results depend on the no-CR reference MC ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md), [L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md), [DELPHI_2006tie](summaries/DELPHI_2006tie.md)). (The earlier L3/DELPHI "tension" is largely a reference-MC effect.)
5. **No model-independent CR observable at hadron colliders.** CR is inferred from tuning ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch), dN/dη, baryon/meson ratios; several models fit these yet disagree on identified-hadron spectra, rapidity gaps and high-n_ch minimum bias ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md), [Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md), [Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md)). Can CR be distinguished from ropes, flow-like dynamics or MPI tuning? (Rope and small-system papers queued.)
6. **CR uncertainty on m_top.** Estimates range from ~0.2 GeV (QCD-based models calibrated on m_W) to ~0.5 GeV (forced/swap/move models) ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md), [Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)). Which tt̄ measurements actually constrain it (ATLAS:2022ctr queued)?
7. **Untested CR predictions.** Area-law CR predicts up to 2× more large rapidity gaps at the Z and Δm_W ≈ +65 MeV ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)); QCD-based CR predicts ~20× more charm/bottom baryons than default ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)). Have SLD gap data or LHC heavy-baryon ratios tested these?
8. **Jet pull and CR.** Jet pull separates colour-singlet from colour-octet W but moves only ≈0.2 %/bin under CR tune variation, and no generator describes all pull observables ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)); jet-pull and dipolarity performance depends on the shower generator ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md), [Hook_2011cq](summaries/Hook_2011cq.md)).

## Colour entanglement / factorization breaking

9. **Colour entanglement is predicted but unseen.** A PHENIX search found widths following standard CSS evolution; an apparent signal was a selection bias; √s-independence vs Drell–Yan is only a hint ([Osborn_2018bwn](summaries/Osborn_2018bwn.md)). Theory papers (Rogers–Mulders, Collins–Qiu) and the PHENIX journal versions are queued.

## Statistical / methodological

10. Most classic coherence measurements are detector-level, shape-only, statistics-limited ([CDF_1994zkl](summaries/CDF_1994zkl.md), [D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md), [OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md), [JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md)); CMS provides unfolded data ([CMS_2013cvx](summaries/CMS_2013cvx.md)). Which coherence/CR measurements are in HEPData/Rivet for generator tests?
11. The D0 W+jet evidence rests on 390 events ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)); CMS Z+2j does not test coherence on/off ([CMS_2021hnp](summaries/CMS_2021hnp.md)). A high-statistics LHC jet–boson coherence measurement seems to be missing.
