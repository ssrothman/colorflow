# Open questions and ideas lacking convincing experimental validation

_Status: after iteration 3 (2026-11-02). Each item cites the summaries that raise it._

## Coherence and the string effect

1. **Perturbative coherence or string? No measurement yet discriminates.** At large N_c the qq̄g antenna reduces to two boosted dipoles, literally the Lund string mechanism ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md), [Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). Every string-effect measurement so far is fitted equally by Lund strings and by angular-ordered showers with cluster hadronization ([JADE_1985mmd](summaries/JADE_1985mmd.md), [OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md), [JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md), [Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)). Proposed discriminants:
   - energy evolution of r and of its hadron-mass / p_out dependence (falls with energy and becomes species-independent with a cascade; stays species-dependent for a pure string) ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md));
   - double-inclusive r₂/r₁ (but ARIADNE's r₂ < r₁ is perturbative spill-over, not the 1/N_c² term, so a measurement would test the cascade's angular spread, not colour-suppressed interference) ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md));
   - 1/N_c² azimuthal asymmetries, unobserved as of 1996 ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
   Khoze et al. read L3/OPAL p_out and ALEPH m_h trends as favouring pQCD, but call nothing decisive ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). Were the r(E_cm), r(M_H, p_out) and r₂/r₁ tests ever done at LEP? (LEP-1 string-effect papers ALEPH:1999ad, Kneringer:1995wh, Duchesneau:1994gi, L3 PLB 345 (1995) 74, OPAL:1995hjp queued.) Has any later measurement seen a 1/N_c² colour effect?
2. **Quantitative agreement of the analytic string-effect prediction is loose.** r_PT = 1.50 (JADE) and 1.55 (TPC) vs data 1.39 ± 0.04 and 1.19 ± 0.05: ~2.8σ and ~7σ below (our arithmetic, stat. only); JADE and TPC differ by ~3σ from each other. The deficit is attributed to a "non-dual" resonance component that is argued, not computed ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)). Similarly, OPAL's C(π) = 0.787 ± 0.002 ± 0.04 matches theory only via an argued ≈ 10 % higher-order correction to ≈ 0.93 ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)). How well does LPHD hold quantitatively ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md): "Achilles heel")?
3. **How strong is the hump-backed-plateau evidence for coherence?** The 91 GeV ξ shape is fitted by an incoherent shower + string; the evidence rests on one slope (b = 0.637 ± 0.016 from five points, two experiments), where coherent and incoherent string models differ by ~1σ in tension with data (our arithmetic), and hadronization shifts b by 0.03–0.07 in every MC ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md)). Boudinov et al. argue hadron spectra are insensitive to coherence in JETSET and that g→gg gluons give b ≈ 0.60 regardless ([Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md)); Khoze et al. concede the point within MCs ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). Counter-points: Boudinov's case rests on one generator with unstated retuning, and coherence strongly suppresses secondary-gluon multiplicity, which they did not examine ([Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md)). Did later analyses (restricted cones per [Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), limiting-spectrum fits, LEP2/HERA ξ* evolution) settle it? Forward citations of OPAL:1990vmr and Boudinov:1993mc not yet run.
4. **Do angular-correlation tests isolate interference?** OPAL TPAC and L3 PPCA exclude incoherent showers with the same string hadronization ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md), [L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md)), but the incoherent shower also differs in cutoff and Λ (Q₀ = 1.45 GeV, Λ = 0.44 GeV), the quoted σ ignore bin correlations, and the azimuthal-asymmetry part of coherence (J1 vs J2) does not survive hadronization ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)). L3's detector correction is derived from coherent MCs only, and HERWIG fails EECA near the peak for unexplained reasons ([L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md)).
5. **Hadron-collider coherence is not fully described.** Tevatron data favoured HERWIG, but at 7 TeV no generator fits the unfolded β distribution, especially forward (best χ²/17 dof ≈ 3.3–3.5), and a tree-level calculation did nearly as well at the Tevatron ([CMS_2013cvx](summaries/CMS_2013cvx.md), [D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md), [CMS_2021hnp](summaries/CMS_2021hnp.md)). Is the forward discrepancy coherence, ISR modelling, or matching?
6. **Size of subleading-colour effects in showers.** Coherent showers drop 1/N² terms ("few percent", shown on two configurations) ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md), [Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md)); dipole cascades drop the negative q–q̄ antenna ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)); SK's perturbative WW pattern has negative "dipoles" with no MC counterpart ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). The colour exactness of BCM's N-gluon closed-chain formula is not demonstrated in their review ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). Beyond-leading-colour shower papers, Catani–Ciafaloni, and BCMM 1982 are queued.

## Colour reconnection

7. **LEP2 CR is favoured but only within one model.** k_I = 1.26 (+0.84 −0.64), no-CR disfavoured at 2.8σ, only SK-I constrained directly; other models enter via a "same reconnection fraction" argument; individual results depend on the no-CR reference MC ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md), [L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md), [DELPHI_2006tie](summaries/DELPHI_2006tie.md)). New from the model papers: SK-II has no free strength and gives consistently negative m_W shifts (−21 to −34 MeV at 170 GeV) while SK-I's are small and of uncertain sign, so an SK-I-based systematic does not automatically cover SK-II ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)); GH criticise SK-I's strength as having "no dynamical motivation" ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)); k_I is implementation-specific ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)).
8. **What sets the reconnection probability?** Estimates span 1/9 from naive counting ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md)); a group-theory bound of only 0.03 < P < 0.97 and (n+1)(m+1) options after hard-gluon emission ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)); ~30 % in SK's reference ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)); ~22 % ARIADNE-2 and ~11 % HERWIG defaults vs LEP's ~51 % ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)); 15–20 % from the B → J/ψ X analogy ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). Is CR driven by space-time overlap (SK) or by string-length minimisation (GH, ARIADNE)? LEP2 has not distinguished them. The type-I vs type-II vacuum question posed in 1988 remains untested model-independently ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)).
9. **Proposed but untested WW observables.** GH's zero-central-particle test (P(0) in |y| < 0.5 55× higher for recoupled events; 13 vs 1.9 events in 5000 at 10 % recoupling) — no LEP2 measurement of it is known to us, and the generator-level estimate ignores detector effects, backgrounds and the tune sensitivity of a ~10⁻³ no-CR P(0); its sensitivity also assumes only the min-λ state recouples ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). Likewise SK's high-statistics ZZ calibration run and BE-vs-topology test ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). Were any done?
10. **Is reconnection partly perturbative?** ARIADNE reconnects after every cascade emission; intra-system CR at the Z hardens p_⊥out by ~10–20 %, which the author says data favour, but "no firm conclusions" without a full retune ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)). SK find perturbative CR negligible in WW ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). Not resolved by the summaries read so far.
11. **No model-independent CR observable at hadron colliders.** CR is inferred from tuning ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch), dN/dη, baryon/meson ratios; several models fit these yet disagree on identified-hadron spectra, rapidity gaps and high-n_ch minimum bias ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md), [Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md), [Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md)). Can CR be distinguished from ropes, flow-like dynamics or MPI tuning? (Rope and small-system papers queued.)
12. **CR uncertainty on m_top.** Estimates range from ~0.2 GeV (QCD-based models calibrated on m_W) to ~0.5 GeV (forced/swap/move models) ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md), [Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)). Which tt̄ measurements actually constrain it (ATLAS:2022ctr queued)?
13. **Untested CR predictions and rapidity gaps.** Area-law CR predicts up to 2× more large rapidity gaps at the Z and Δm_W ≈ +65 MeV ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)); QCD-based CR predicts ~20× more charm/bottom baryons ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)). At HERA, CR in ARIADNE's unordered cascade gives far too few gaps, while SCI-type CR with ordered showers is not excluded (MC-vs-MC only) ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)). Have SLD/LEP gap data or LHC heavy-baryon ratios tested these?
14. **Jet pull and CR.** Jet pull separates colour-singlet from colour-octet W but moves only ≈0.2 %/bin under CR tune variation, and no generator describes all pull observables ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)); jet-pull and dipolarity performance depends on the shower generator ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md), [Hook_2011cq](summaries/Hook_2011cq.md)).

## Colour entanglement / factorization breaking

15. **Colour entanglement is predicted but unseen.** A PHENIX search found widths following standard CSS evolution; an apparent signal was a selection bias; √s-independence vs Drell–Yan is only a hint ([Osborn_2018bwn](summaries/Osborn_2018bwn.md)). Theory papers (Rogers–Mulders, Collins–Qiu) and the PHENIX journal versions are queued.

## Statistical / methodological

16. Most classic coherence measurements are detector-level or bin-by-bin corrected, shape-only, statistics-limited, and often published only as figures without tables or covariances ([CDF_1994zkl](summaries/CDF_1994zkl.md), [D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md), [OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md), [JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md), [JADE_1985mmd](summaries/JADE_1985mmd.md), [L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md), [OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)); CMS provides unfolded data ([CMS_2013cvx](summaries/CMS_2013cvx.md)). Which coherence/CR measurements are in HEPData/Rivet for generator tests?
17. The D0 W+jet evidence rests on 390 events ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)); CMS Z+2j does not test coherence on/off ([CMS_2021hnp](summaries/CMS_2021hnp.md)). A high-statistics LHC jet–boson coherence measurement seems to be missing.
18. Coherent-vs-incoherent MC comparisons are rarely like-for-like: incoherent options are retuned only to ⟨n_ch⟩ or not stated to be retuned, and cascade vs string-only setups use different fragmentation parameters ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md), [Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md), [L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md), [Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)). Conclusions about "coherence on/off" are therefore conditional on the tune.
