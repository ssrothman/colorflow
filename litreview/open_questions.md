# Open questions and ideas lacking convincing experimental validation

_Status: after iteration 4 (2026-11-02). Each item cites the summaries that raise it._

## Coherence and the string effect

1. **Perturbative coherence or string? No measurement yet discriminates.** At large N_c the qq̄g antenna reduces to two boosted dipoles, literally the Lund string mechanism ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md), [Andersson_1980vk](summaries/Andersson_1980vk.md), [Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). Every string-effect measurement so far is fitted equally by Lund strings and by angular-ordered showers with cluster hadronization ([JADE_1985mmd](summaries/JADE_1985mmd.md), [OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md), [JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md), [Andersson_1983ia](summaries/Andersson_1983ia.md)). Proposed discriminants:
   - energy evolution of r and of its hadron-mass / p_out dependence (falls with energy and becomes species-independent with a cascade; stays species-dependent for a pure string) ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md));
   - double-inclusive r₂/r₁ (but ARIADNE's r₂ < r₁ is perturbative spill-over, not the 1/N_c² term, so a measurement would test the cascade's angular spread, not colour-suppressed interference) ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md));
   - 1/N_c² azimuthal asymmetries, unobserved as of 1996 ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)).
   Khoze et al. read L3/OPAL p_out and ALEPH m_h trends as favouring pQCD, but call nothing decisive ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). Were the r(E_cm), r(M_H, p_out) and r₂/r₁ tests ever done at LEP? (LEP-1 string-effect papers ALEPH:1999ad, Kneringer:1995wh, Duchesneau:1994gi, L3 PLB 345 (1995) 74, OPAL:1995hjp queued.) Has any later measurement seen a 1/N_c² colour effect?
2. **Quantitative agreement of the analytic string-effect prediction is loose.** r_PT = 1.50 (JADE) and 1.55 (TPC) vs data 1.39 ± 0.04 and 1.19 ± 0.05: ~2.8σ and ~7σ below (our arithmetic, stat. only); JADE and TPC differ by ~3σ from each other. The deficit is attributed to a "non-dual" resonance component that is argued, not computed ([Azimov_1985zta](summaries/Azimov_1985zta.md)). Similarly, OPAL's C(π) = 0.787 ± 0.002 ± 0.04 matches theory only via an argued ≈ 10 % higher-order correction to ≈ 0.93 ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)). How well does LPHD hold quantitatively ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md): "Achilles heel")?
3. **How strong is the hump-backed-plateau evidence for coherence?** The 91 GeV ξ shape is fitted by an incoherent shower + string; the evidence rests on one slope (b = 0.637 ± 0.016 from five points, two experiments), where coherent and incoherent string models differ by ~1σ in tension with data (our arithmetic), and hadronization shifts b by 0.03–0.07 in every MC ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md)). Boudinov et al. argue hadron spectra are insensitive to coherence in JETSET and that g→gg gluons give b ≈ 0.60 regardless ([Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md)); Khoze et al. concede the point within MCs ([Khoze_1996dn](summaries/Khoze_1996dn.md)). Counter-points: Boudinov's case rests on one generator with unstated retuning, and coherence strongly suppresses secondary-gluon multiplicity, which they did not examine ([Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md)). Did later analyses (restricted cones per [Dokshitzer_1987nm](summaries/Dokshitzer_1987nm.md), limiting-spectrum fits, LEP2/HERA ξ* evolution) settle it? Forward citations of OPAL:1990vmr and Boudinov:1993mc not yet run. A direct coherent-vs-non-coherent shower comparison (Bengtsson:1986et) is blocked; see the gaps list.
4. **Do angular-correlation tests isolate interference?** OPAL TPAC and L3 PPCA exclude incoherent showers with the same string hadronization ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md), [L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md)), but the incoherent shower also differs in cutoff and Λ (Q₀ = 1.45 GeV, Λ = 0.44 GeV), the quoted σ ignore bin correlations, and the azimuthal-asymmetry part of coherence (J1 vs J2) does not survive hadronization ([OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)). L3's detector correction is derived from coherent MCs only, and HERWIG fails EECA near the peak for unexplained reasons ([L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md)).
5. **Hadron-collider coherence is not fully described.** Tevatron data favoured HERWIG, but at 7 TeV no generator fits the unfolded β distribution, especially forward (best χ²/17 dof ≈ 3.3–3.5), and a tree-level calculation did nearly as well at the Tevatron ([CMS_2013cvx](summaries/CMS_2013cvx.md), [D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md), [CMS_2021hnp](summaries/CMS_2021hnp.md)). Is the forward discrepancy coherence, ISR modelling, or matching?
6. **Size of subleading-colour effects in showers.** Coherent showers drop 1/N² terms ("few percent", shown on two configurations) ([Marchesini_1987cf](summaries/Marchesini_1987cf.md), [Ellis_1986bv](summaries/Ellis_1986bv.md)); dipole cascades drop the negative q–q̄ antenna ([Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)); SK's perturbative WW pattern has negative "dipoles" with no MC counterpart ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). The colour exactness of BCM's N-gluon closed-chain formula is not demonstrated in their review ([Bassetto_1983mvz](summaries/Bassetto_1983mvz.md)). Beyond-leading-colour shower papers, Catani–Ciafaloni, and BCMM 1982 are queued.

## Colour reconnection

7. **LEP2 CR is favoured but only within one model.** k_I = 1.26 (+0.84 −0.64), no-CR disfavoured at 2.8σ, only SK-I constrained directly; other models enter via a "same reconnection fraction" argument; individual results depend on the no-CR reference MC ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md), [L3_2003oci](summaries/L3_2003oci.md), [DELPHI_2006tie](summaries/DELPHI_2006tie.md)). OPAL's particle flow alone excludes only ~100 % SK-I (4.4σ) and has expected sensitivity ≤ 1.3σ_stat to SK-II/II′, AR-2 and HERWIG-CR; its SK-I k_I depends on the shower cut-off Q₀ and on √s, so raw k_I values "cannot be directly compared" across experiments ([OPAL_2005cdr](summaries/OPAL_2005cdr.md)). SK-II has no free strength and gives consistently negative m_W shifts (−21 to −34 MeV at 170 GeV) while SK-I's are small and of uncertain sign, so an SK-I-based systematic does not automatically cover SK-II ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)); GH criticise SK-I's strength as having "no dynamical motivation" ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)); k_I is implementation-specific ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). Against LHC-era models the LEP2 particle flow excludes only GM-I (3.9σ) ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)).
8. **What sets the reconnection probability?** Estimates span 1/9 from naive counting ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md)); a group-theory bound of only 0.03 < P < 0.97 and (n+1)(m+1) options after hard-gluon emission ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)); ~30 % in SK's reference ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)); ~22 % ARIADNE-2 and ~11 % HERWIG defaults vs LEP's ~51 % ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)); OPAL's SK-I fit 37 % (10–56 % at 68 % CL) ([OPAL_2005cdr](summaries/OPAL_2005cdr.md)); 15–20 % from the B → J/ψ X analogy ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). Soft-gluon evolution gives an event-dependent p_rec between ≈ 0 and ≈ 0.9 in toy cluster systems (1/10 and 9/10 at minimal/maximal mixing for two clusters), with μ and α_s as free parameters ([Gieseke_2018gff](summaries/Gieseke_2018gff.md)). Is CR driven by space-time overlap (SK) or by string-length minimisation (GH, ARIADNE)? LEP2 has not distinguished them. The type-I vs type-II vacuum question posed in 1988 remains untested model-independently ([Gustafson_1988fs](summaries/Gustafson_1988fs.md), [ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)).
9. **Proposed but untested WW observables.** GH's zero-central-particle test (P(0) in |y| < 0.5 55× higher for recoupled events; 13 vs 1.9 events in 5000 at 10 % recoupling) — no LEP2 measurement of it is known to us, and the generator-level estimate ignores detector effects, backgrounds and the tune sensitivity of a ~10⁻³ no-CR P(0); its sensitivity also assumes only the min-λ state recouples ([Gustafson_1994cd](summaries/Gustafson_1994cd.md)). Likewise SK's high-statistics ZZ calibration run and BE-vs-topology test ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). Were any done? (The qqqq vs lνqq multiplicity comparison was done and is insensitive at LEP precision ([OPAL_2005cdr](summaries/OPAL_2005cdr.md)).) Junction–antijunction CR in WW (back-to-back baryon–antibaryon pairs) was never modelled in LEP-era CR ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)).
10. **Is reconnection partly perturbative?** ARIADNE reconnects after every cascade emission; intra-system CR at the Z hardens p_⊥out by ~10–20 %, which the author says data favour, but "no firm conclusions" without a full retune ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)). SK find perturbative CR negligible in WW ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). Gieseke et al. derive CR-like colour mixing from one-loop soft-gluon evolution and argue it is "not simply a 1/N suppressed effect", but extrapolate below the shower cut-off and test only toy cluster ensembles; the model was not implemented in Herwig by late 2019 ([Gieseke_2018gff](summaries/Gieseke_2018gff.md), [Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)). Has an implementation been confronted with data?
11. **No model-independent CR observable at hadron colliders.** CR is inferred from tuning ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch), dN/dη, baryon/meson ratios; several models fit these yet disagree on identified-hadron spectra, rapidity gaps and high-n_ch minimum bias ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md), [Gieseke_2012ft](summaries/Gieseke_2012ft.md), [Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md), [Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md), [Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)). The founding ⟨p⊥⟩(n_ch) argument compares with Tune A rather than data and shows only that *some* final-state colour correlation reducing string length is needed within the MPI + string picture; the authors explicitly deny it is evidence for CR ([Sjostrand_2004pf](summaries/Sjostrand_2004pf.md), [Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)). CR strength is degenerate with MPI parameters (p⊥0, m₀): spatially constraining CR flattens ⟨p⊥⟩(N_ch) until a retune restores it at the cost of the N_ch tail and p⊥ spectrum ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)). Ropes, junction CR and MPI-based CR each describe part of the identified-hadron data (items 20–22). Which observable would separate CR from ropes or flow-like dynamics?
12. **CR uncertainty on m_top: still a model spread, not a measurement.** Estimates range from ~0.15 GeV (Pythia 8.1 CR on/off) and ~0.2 GeV (QCD-based models calibrated on m_W) to ~0.5 GeV (forced/swap/move models; the 2007 Tevatron within-class tune spread) and "up to 400 MeV" quoted by ATLAS from CMS ([Argyropoulos_2014zoa](summaries/Argyropoulos_2014zoa.md), [Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md), [Sjostrand_2013cya](summaries/Sjostrand_2013cya.md), [Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md), [ATLAS_2022ctr](summaries/ATLAS_2022ctr.md)). The 2007 ±0.5 GeV lumps CR with UE, beam-remnant and IR parameters in a generator-level toy ([Skands_2007zg](summaries/Skands_2007zg.md)). The ATLAS tt̄ measurement disfavours no-CR but barely responds to the top-specific TCR models, because jets (most top-decay products) are excluded ([ATLAS_2022ctr](summaries/ATLAS_2022ctr.md)). Which measurement constrains reconnection involving the b and W decay products? (CMS tt̄ UE / CR-tune papers 1807.02810, 2205.02905 are leads.)
13. **Untested CR predictions and rapidity gaps.** Area-law CR predicts up to 2× more large rapidity gaps at the Z (event-level, Δy > 6) and Δm_W ≈ +65 MeV ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md)); the LEP-1 gluon-jet gap tests disfavour GAL at its default strength (items 15–17), but the Rathsman event-level large-gap prediction itself has not been checked against SLD/LEP data in the summaries read. QCD-based CR predicts strongly enhanced heavy baryons (Λc, Λb about ×2, Σc, Σb about ×20–30 relative to old CR) ([Christiansen_2015yqa](summaries/Christiansen_2015yqa.md)); LHC Λc⁺/D⁰ now agrees while Λb⁰/B⁰ is overshot (item 21). At HERA, CR in ARIADNE's unordered cascade gives far too few gaps, while SCI-type CR with ordered showers is not excluded (MC-vs-MC only) ([Lonnblad_1995yk](summaries/Lonnblad_1995yk.md)); the SCI letter's own HERA comparison is qualitative ([Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md)).
14. **Jet pull and CR.** Jet pull separates colour-singlet from colour-octet W but moves only ≈0.2 %/bin under CR tune variation, and no generator describes all pull observables ([ATLAS_2018olo](summaries/ATLAS_2018olo.md)); jet-pull and dipolarity performance depends on the shower generator ([Gallicchio_2010sw](summaries/Gallicchio_2010sw.md), [Hook_2011cq](summaries/Hook_2011cq.md)).
15. **Is soft colour rearrangement universal? HERA vs LEP-1.** GAL's default R₀ = 0.10 was fixed on HERA diffractive DIS, and SCI uses R = 0.2 for DIS gaps; OPAL notes Rathsman's model with R₀ = 0.1 also describes ep and pp̄ gaps ([Rathsman_1998tp](summaries/Rathsman_1998tp.md), [Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md), [OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md)). Inside a single Z → qq̄g system, LEP-1 wants R₀ ≈ 0.01–0.02 (ALEPH) or R₀ = 0.0085 ± 0.0075 ± 0.0087, consistent with zero (OPAL) ([ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md), [OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md)). Is the difference due to the hadron remnant (a mechanism absent in e⁺e⁻, OPAL's suggestion), to different shower cut-offs (the LEP R₀ is tied to Q₀ = 1.5 GeV, and the HERA gap rate depends strongly on Q₀² and y_cut), or to a genuine non-universality? SCI and GAL also use different reconnection criteria, so the comparison is not like-for-like ([ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md), [Edin_1995gi](summaries/Edin_1995gi.md)). The same question arises for LEP2, where ~50 % inter-W reconnection is preferred ([ALEPH_2013dgf](summaries/ALEPH_2013dgf.md)).
16. **What explains the excess of neutral gap gluon jets over no-CR strings?** No tested model describes LEP-1 gluon jets with a rapidity gap: non-CR JETSET/ARIADNE undershoot by ~10–20 % (jet 3) and ~30–50 % (b-tagged gluons), default CR overshoots ([ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md)), and OPAL finds the same pattern (Jetset/Ariadne ~20 % low at Q_leading = 0) ([OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md)). A small CR strength, colour-octet neutralisation (Minkowski–Ochs) or gluon-jet fragmentation deficiencies could all explain it; the glueball search shows only ~2σ excesses ([OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md), [ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md)).
17. **The LEP-1 gluon-jet gap test has not been applied to modern models.** Pythia 8 QCD-CR (junctions) and gluon-move, Herwig 7 plain/statistical/baryonic/spacetime CR, and ropes all produce isolated colour-singlet gluon systems at some rate, and the even-n_ch spikes / Q_leading = 0 signature is sharp ([OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md)). Default Ariadne-CR/GAL fail; HERWIG cluster CR is insensitive or inconclusive ([ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md), [OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md)). OPAL's hadron-level distributions are unfolded with ~40 % Herwig-based corrections; whether a HEPData/Rivet record exists was not checked ([OPAL_2003njc](summaries/OPAL_2003njc.md)). Forward-citation runs (`citing 620566`, `citing 715094`) are pending. Could a Z-pole run at a future e⁺e⁻ collider redo this with modern unfolding?
18. **Future e⁺e⁻: model-dependent sign of δm_W and untested proposals.** At 170 GeV the Pythia 8 models give m_W shifts from −41 MeV (GM-I) to +49 MeV (GM-II); at 240 GeV GM-II reaches ≈ +370 to +400 MeV while CS stays within ≈ +10 MeV ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)). Proposed tests — m_W(4q) − m_W(2qℓν) at 240 GeV, the multiplicity ratio (2 without CR, 1.93–1.97 with), H → WW → 4q parity angles (CR ~ 2–5 % CP-odd admixture), CR at the tt̄ threshold — come without uncertainties or detector effects, and the Higgs study shows only one angle's χ² ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)). Ropes are explicitly excluded from that model set ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)). FCC-ee/CEPC CR studies (forward citations) not yet read.

## Small-system collectivity: CR, ropes, junctions

19. **Does CR alone produce collective flow in pp?** Pythia MPI-based CR gives radial-flow-like, mass-ordered ⟨p_T⟩(N_ch) and a p/π bump in MC; v₂/v₃ from CR is called "very speculative", and the heuristic string-length measure used for that argument is not Pythia's actual λ ([OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg](summaries/OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg.md)). The rope paper lists plasma, rope tension, CR into low multiplets and rope pressure as candidate sources ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)); the spatially constrained CR and Herwig spacetime CR papers compute no flow observables ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md), [Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)). Has any paper computed v₂ or the ridge from CR alone and compared it with data? (String shoving, Bierlich:2017vhg, and ALICE/CMS identified-particle papers queued.)
20. **Strangeness (Ξ, Ω, Ξc) is not reproduced by CR or junctions.** QCD-CR + junctions underpredicts Ξ/Λ (theory/data ≈ 0.7–0.8, lower than Monash) and Ξc ratios (Ξc⁰/D⁰ theory/data ≈ 0.2–0.4) ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)); Ξ/π and Ω/π stay well below ALICE in the Angantyr spatially constrained CR tune ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)). Ropes describe min-bias Ξ⁻/Λ but were not compared multiplicity-binned and fail the high-p⊥ fall of Λ/K⁰_S ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)). Is a combination (junction CR + ropes) consistent with all ratios, and is the rope high-p⊥ failure due to the rope concept or to DIPSY's too-hard spectrum ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md))?
21. **Junction CR overpredicts Λb⁰/B⁰ (≈ 2×) and p/π.** The same model that reproduces ALICE Λc⁺/D⁰ overshoots LHCb Λb⁰/B⁰ by about a factor 2 at low p⊥ and does not reproduce the similar p⊥ shapes of Λc/D⁰ and Λb/B⁰ in data; the authors cannot say whether Λb is too high or B⁰ too low ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)). In the Angantyr spatially constrained QCD-CR tune, p/π is ≈ 2× ALICE in pp and pPb ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)). Both junction tunes are preliminary (spin-1 diquark parameter set by eye; hand tune of four parameters). What controls b- vs c-baryon production in junction CR?
22. **Mixed-mechanism attribution of the flow-like baryon/meson rise.** Rope authors attribute part of the low-p⊥ p/π and Λ/K rise to the final-state swing (a CR) and part to rope tension, without separating them ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)); Ortiz Velasquez et al. attribute it to MPI-based CR boosts ([OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg](summaries/OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg.md)); junction CR changes the baryon/meson pattern again ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md), [Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md)). Proposed discriminants (baryon–antibaryon correlation strength for junction vs diquark vs popcorn baryons; high-p_T fall-off of CR effects; mass-ordered ⟨p_T⟩ vs N_MPI) have no quantitative predictions yet ([Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md), [OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg](summaries/OrtizVelasquez_2013ofg.md)).
23. **What is the spatial range of CR?** Herwig spacetime CR tunes d₀ = 0.15 fm, smaller than the proton radius, with ν² weakly constrained and data largely insensitive to both ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)); Angantyr's δb_c = 0.5 fm is a pp/pA compromise, and 1 fm already removes the spatial effect in pp ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)); ropes use r₀ ≈ 1 fm ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md)); SK used space-time overlap of flux tubes ([Sjostrand_1993hi](summaries/Sjostrand_1993hi.md)). All use transverse positions only. Is there an observable that measures the CR range, e.g. the proposed Bose–Einstein correlations vs emission position ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)), or the centrality dependence of multiplicity in AA, where near-global CR changes PbPb multiplicity by ≈ −10 % ([Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md))?

## CR in tt̄: degeneracy with MPI

24. **CR vs MPI/UE degeneracy in tt̄ observables.** In the ATLAS 13 TeV tt̄ measurement, data prefer a lower MPI p_T0^ref than A14 as clearly as they disfavour no-CR; SHERPA (no CR) gives *fewer* particles than data, and in HERWIG the UE "compensates" for switching CR off; χ² values change by up to an order of magnitude with the treatment of modelling correlations, and noCR "excluded" / maxCR "compatible" are shown only visually ([ATLAS_2022ctr](summaries/ATLAS_2022ctr.md)). The authors call for simultaneous CR + MPI tuning. Has such a tune (with ATLAS_2022_I2152933 in Rivet) been made, and does it narrow the CR envelope on m_top?

## Colour entanglement / factorization breaking

25. **Colour entanglement is predicted but unseen.** A PHENIX search found widths following standard CSS evolution; an apparent signal was a selection bias; √s-independence vs Drell–Yan is only a hint ([Osborn_2018bwn](summaries/Osborn_2018bwn.md)). Theory papers (Rogers–Mulders, Collins–Qiu) and the PHENIX journal versions are queued.

## Statistical / methodological

26. Most classic coherence measurements are detector-level or bin-by-bin corrected, shape-only, statistics-limited, and often published only as figures without tables or covariances ([CDF_1994zkl](summaries/CDF_1994zkl.md), [D0_1997tye](summaries/D0_1997tye.md), [OPAL_1991xwi](summaries/OPAL_1991xwi.md), [JADE_1981ofk](summaries/JADE_1981ofk.md), [JADE_1985mmd](summaries/JADE_1985mmd.md), [L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md), [OPAL_1992lzb](summaries/OPAL_1992lzb.md)); CMS provides unfolded data ([CMS_2013cvx](summaries/CMS_2013cvx.md)). The same holds for CR: the OPAL WW particle flow is detector-level, tied to OPAL software, and cannot be reused for modern CR models ([OPAL_2005cdr](summaries/OPAL_2005cdr.md)); the LEP combination publishes no separate r ([Christiansen_2015yca](summaries/Christiansen_2015yca.md)); ALEPH's gluon-jet gap significances are statistical only ([ALEPH_2006jcu](summaries/ALEPH_2006jcu.md)). ATLAS tt̄ CR observables are in HEPData/Rivet ([ATLAS_2022ctr](summaries/ATLAS_2022ctr.md)). Which other coherence/CR measurements are in HEPData/Rivet for generator tests?
27. The D0 W+jet evidence rests on 390 events ([D0_1999lfo](summaries/D0_1999lfo.md)); CMS Z+2j does not test coherence on/off ([CMS_2021hnp](summaries/CMS_2021hnp.md)). A high-statistics LHC jet–boson coherence measurement seems to be missing.
28. Coherent-vs-incoherent MC comparisons are rarely like-for-like: incoherent options are retuned only to ⟨n_ch⟩ or not stated to be retuned, and cascade vs string-only setups use different fragmentation parameters ([OPAL_1990vmr](summaries/OPAL_1990vmr.md), [Boudinov_1993mc](summaries/Boudinov_1993mc.md), [L3_1995ahv](summaries/L3_1995ahv.md), [Khoze_1989km](summaries/Khoze_1989km.md)). Conclusions about "coherence on/off" are therefore conditional on the tune. The same applies to CR on/off: model papers in small systems compare by eye with untuned or hand-tuned parameters and no χ² ([Bierlich_2014xba](summaries/Bierlich_2014xba.md), [Altmann_2024odn](summaries/Altmann_2024odn.md), [Lonnblad_2023stc](summaries/Lonnblad_2023stc.md)), and the Herwig spacetime CR paper shows no default-tune curves for comparison ([Bellm_2019wrh](summaries/Bellm_2019wrh.md)).
